Welcome to the Arcanthys wiki.

This is a reference for building characters: continents, peoples, factions, gods, magic, and history, all as they are known within the world itself. The pages here represent public knowledge in Arcanthys. A lot of that knowledge is esoteric, so this wiki should not be treated as a guide to what any particular character actually knows.

Spoilers have been scrubbed as thoroughly as possible, with the closest attention given to the largest lore pages. Even so, some leftover TODOs or spoiler fragments may remain on smaller pages.

## Overview

**Arcanthys** is a world where life and magic share a single source, where an empire's ambition once nearly burned both away, and where every convenience born of magic still carries the risk of its own collapse.

Magic is not rare in Arcanthys. It lights streets, powers engines, drives industry, and shapes daily life. That convenience exists alongside deep instability. The same energy that heals, builds, and illuminates can also warp flesh, corrupt landscapes, and unmake entire cities.

This wiki catalogs the world's continents, peoples, powers, and history. For a fuller introduction to the setting, read the [[A Summary of Arcanthys|Summary of Arcanthys]].
