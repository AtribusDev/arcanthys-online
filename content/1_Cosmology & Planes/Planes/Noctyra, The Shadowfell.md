**Noctyra**, also known as **the Shadowfell**, is the [[Planes|plane]] of the dead and undead and the default destination of mortal souls. Souls drawn into Noctyra are carried into [[The Everdeep|the Everdeep]], where they are stripped of identity. Those that escape the pull of the Everdeep turn into shades and other undead that roam in search of life.

As an [[Inner Planes|inner plane]], Noctyra sits closer to the [[Material Plane]] than most outer realms. During periods of high resonance, this proximity lets spirits, shades, and shadow phenomena slip into mortal regions with unusual frequency.

# Description
Noctyra is shaped by two major regions. The Hollow Dark forms most of the plane, while the Everdeep runs through its center.

Visitors to either region are worn down the longer they stay. Colors fade toward grey, sound goes muffled and flat, and hope, resolve, and joy drain away under the plane's negative energy.

# Regions
## The Everdeep
The Everdeep is a vast, slow-moving swamp-river of soul sludge where most of the dead eventually settle and disperse. Its drag wears down the body and mind alike, pulling at memory and sense of self the longer a creature remains caught in it. A muted green glow lights the sludge in a faint, sourceless twilight, enough to see by, but offering neither warmth nor any reliable sense of direction.

The pull of the Everdeep is strong and dangerous. Only the most powerful magics offer a reliable means of escape.

## The Hollow Dark
The Hollow Dark holds no natural light. No sun, star, or ambient flame burns there by default, and any light carried in is badly dimmed. Shadows ripple and shift once they form. The land itself seems restless: twisted forests, jagged spires, and half-collapsed ruins shift and rearrange around anyone who lingers. Fear and paranoia grow sharper in the living the longer they remain.

Old cruelties do not fade here: they echo. Shades and spirits that linger long enough in the Hollow Dark stop merely reliving what was done to them, and begin re-enacting it on whatever wanders too close.

## Undeath and Noctyra
Undead in Arcanthys are not alive; they are sustained instead by Noctyra's negative energy, which holds back the decay, rest, and dispersal that would otherwise claim them.

Undead that cling to fragments of soul torn from the Everdeep are often twisted in mind and memory. Long dependence on negative energy tends to leave them fixated on the living, showing itself as predation, compulsion, or a warped kind of attachment.

# Planar Interactions
Noctyra is sustained by the emotions that accompany death: sadness, grief, despair, fear, and malice. These are not spread evenly. Grief and apathy run strongest in the Everdeep; despair, fear, and malice run strongest in the Hollow Dark.

Unlike most planes, Noctyra rarely simply overflows with power. Instead it deepens by region: a surge of death and despair thickens the Hollow Dark first, and only then strengthens the pull of the Everdeep.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Noctyra becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the boundary thins unevenly, depending on local conditions. In places heavy with grief, loss, and apathy, the Everdeep's touch shows through: spirits cross over more easily, and resurrection magic grows stronger, though far less predictable.

In places thick with fear and despair, the Hollow Dark shows through instead: the darkness deepens, shadows spread further than they should, and paranoia takes root among anyone already vulnerable to it.

When the resonance grows strong enough, pieces of Noctyra itself bleed into the Material Plane. For as long as it lasts, the ground and air take on the plane's alien qualities, until the boundary settles and the world reasserts itself.

Necromancy and other negative-energy workings come easier in these resonant zones, though the ease is paired with instability and side effects that are harder to predict.

### Quiescent
When Noctyra falls quiet, its pull on the world eases. Spirits cross over less often, resurrection magic grows harder to work, and pathways of shadow close or fall dormant.

Undead sustained by Noctyra's currents may weaken during long quiet periods, particularly in places where death's touch was never especially strong to begin with.
