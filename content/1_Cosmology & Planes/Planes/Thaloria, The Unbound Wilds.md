**Thaloria, the Unbound Wilds**, is the [[Planes|plane]] of life without restraint, where growth, hunger, and instinct answer to nothing but themselves. No hand tends Thaloria's wilds, and none has ever held them for long.

# Description
Thaloria holds every kind of wilderness the Material Plane knows, but never at once and never in the same place twice. A given stretch of ground may stand as dense jungle for a time, and later as tundra, desert, or flooded marsh, without any of these states lasting. Nothing here is cultivated. Growth reaches sizes and densities that would be impossible under any tended sky, and every living thing competes for the same ground, the same light, and the same prey.

## The Turning
Thaloria's climate does not change with any season. Instead, vast fronts of climate sweep across the plane in a slow, unbroken rotation, called the Turning by those who study it. Jungle rots into frost. Frost cracks into desert. Desert floods into wetland. The Turning follows no fixed path, order, or duration, and no record exists of it ever stopping.

The change does not begin with the landscape. Before the ground itself turns, the air thins or thickens, light shifts in color, and pressure builds in a way that can be felt on the skin. Animals fall silent. Plants droop or curl inward. Anything that survives Thaloria for long learns to recognize these signs, because by the time the biome itself begins to change, it is often too late to react. Most inhabitants either migrate with the Turning or hibernate, waiting out an unfavorable phase in safety.

## The Hunt
Thaloria's abundance of plant life supports an equally abundant population of grazing and foraging creatures, which in turn sustains a predator population dense enough that no niche in the food chain goes unfilled. At its uppermost tier, that chain includes both instinct-driven beasts and monstrous, carnivorous [[Sentient Plants]], which compete for the same kills rather than existing beneath them.

### Death and Growth
Every living thing in Arcanthys carries [[Lumir]], but in Thaloria, a creature's death does not let that essence dissipate quietly. At the moment of a kill, the victim's Lumir bursts outward as a brief, concentrated surge that anything nearby, root or claw, can draw upon. Predators that feed near enough to a kill take on a fleeting surge of vitality. Kill sites are also where Thaloria's plant life grows fastest, and it is not unusual for a carcass to vanish beneath new, unnaturally rapid growth within a single day.

This surge is at its strongest along the collapsing edge of the Turning, where mass death among stragglers and hibernators caught unprepared produces the richest concentrations of released Lumir anywhere on the plane. Skilled hunters, native or otherwise, do not hunt at random. They track the Turning itself, because the wavefront is reliably where the richest kills, and the richest Lumir, can be found.

# Planar Interactions
Thaloria is sustained by instinct and freedom, the pull of any living thing acting on impulse rather than restraint, and any impulse that breaks free of imposed order or control.

When Thaloria accumulates excess emotional energy, it becomes increasingly resonant. Growth accelerates further, predatory instinct sharpens, and the boundary that keeps the Turning's phases distinct grows less stable.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Thaloria becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], wild growth on the Material Plane accelerates beyond anything cultivation can account for, and beasts across the region grow noticeably more aggressive and territorial. In the most heavily affected regions, migrations and short-lived climate shifts echoing the Turning have been reported, vanishing once resonance recedes.

### Quiescent
When Thaloria is quiescent, wild growth slows to ordinary rates, beasts grow easier to manage or domesticate, and cultivated land holds its shape without contest.
