**Varynth, the Rising Labyrinth**, is the [[Planes|plane]] of endless ascent. It embodies not conquest, nor greed, nor pride alone, but the hunger to rise: to improve, to surpass, to become more than what one was. Its inhabitants face a seemingly endless climb.

# Description
Varynth is a single, immense vertical megastructure suspended within a lightless firmament. There is no sky, no ground, no stars, only the structure itself. It stretches upward and downward without a known end.

The plane is composed entirely of architecture: colossal stairways spiralling into darkness, suspended bridges vanishing into mist, cavernous chambers carved at improbable angles, vertical shafts miles wide, and tiered districts stacked one atop another.

Damage to the structure is repaired in time by mechanical workers, known as the [[Elevated]]. By their magic the structure reforms itself. Crumbled staircases mend. Fallen bridges knit themselves whole. Shattered chambers rebuild.

The architecture obeys no stable geometry:
- A chamber may be vast within but appear narrow from the outside.
- A stair climbed twice may lead to different destinations.
- Corridors may loop back on themselves or return to places that should not exist.
- Entire tiers subtly rearrange when unobserved.

Varynth is unpredictable. It is a vertical labyrinth of perpetual reconfiguration. All creatures within feel a subtle, constant pull upward.

## The Law of Ascent
In Varynth, progress has an enforced direction. Any creature that willingly descends more than a few feet begins to suffer Degradation, an erosion of self proportional to the depth of descent.

Effects include:
- Vertigo and spatial disorientation
- Progressive exhaustion
- Weakening of physical or magical capability
- Memory erosion
- Loss of identity or ambition
- Physical corruption
- Death

The deeper one descends, the more severe the Degradation becomes. No creature appears immune, not even undead or constructs, except for the [[Elevated]].

# Inhabitants
- The [[Elevated]], construct beings of unknown origin whose forms mirror the geometry of Varynth itself. They neither hinder nor assist climbers without purpose; they only tend to the path.

# Planar Interactions
Varynth is fuelled by ambition, pride, aspiration, and obsession: the want to surpass one's former self and take pride in what has been achieved.

When this emotional current swells, the plane grows increasingly resonant. The pull upward becomes nearly irresistible.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Varynth becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the [[Material Plane]] feels the pressure of aspiration.

Ambition sharpens into obsession. Rivalries intensify. Scholars refuse to abandon unsolved problems. Kings pursue expansion beyond reason. Apprentices challenge masters prematurely.

Magic that enhances the self (divination, augmentation, transformation) grows stronger and more precise, whereas teleportation magic becomes unreliable.

### Quiescent
When Varynth is quiescent, ambition softens. Rivalries cool. Perfection feels unnecessary. Mortals accept limits more easily.
