**Arcanum** is the sun of [[Arcanthys]], the source of the light and warmth that sustain the world. It shares its name with [[Arcanum (Creator God of Myth)|Arcanum]], the creator god of legend credited with its formation, and its light is held to be the wellspring of [[Lumir]], the life essence that flows through all living things.

# Name and the God of Myth
Arcanum's name is inseparable from the god of the same name. According to established tradition, the god Arcanum gave his own essence to the barren world of Arcanthys during the [[Age of Shaping]], and that essence became the sun, a lasting memorial to his sacrifice.

Not everyone accepts this order of events. Some hold that the sun was known and named long before the god was given form in worship, and that Arcanum the god was named after Arcanum the sun, in recognition of the life and Lumir its rays provide. No surviving record settles the matter, and both accounts are taught side by side in different traditions. See [[Arcanum (Creator God of Myth)]] for the full mythology.

# Wellspring of Lumir
Lumir, the life essence that fuels life and magic across Arcanthys, is said to originate from Arcanum and to radiate outward from it, spreading across the [[Material Plane]] to Arcanthys and, through it, to the other [[Planes]]. The sun's rays are described as the means by which Lumir is carried to and guided toward Arcanthys.

The [[Cycle of the Soul]] ties souls to this same radiance: conscious essence is said to form within the light of Arcanum as it falls upon Arcanthys, descending from there into the Material Plane to bind itself to living creatures. Some accounts hold that dispersed spiritual essence eventually returns to form new souls beneath Arcanum's light, continuing the cycle.

# Position within the Material Plane
Arcanum lies within the [[Material Plane]] alongside Arcanthys and its satellites. The Material Plane itself is enfolded by the [[Astral Plane]] and the [[Ethereal Plane]].
