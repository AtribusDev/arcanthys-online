**Gildara, the Boundless Embrace**, is the [[Planes|plane]] of love, compassion, and enforced unity. It embodies connection without boundary and the belief that suffering is best resolved through shared experience rather than individual endurance.

Where Rathune perpetuates division and conflict, Gildara eliminates division entirely. It does not cultivate harmony through agreement, but through integration. Within its domain, isolation is regarded as the fundamental cause of suffering.

# Description
Gildara is a vast, living biosphere of interwoven forests, root-plains, and organic structures bound together by an immense arboreal network, lit by a diffuse, radiant gold light that emanates from its center and reflects across bark, leaf, and soil alike. At its heart stands [[The Heartloom]], a colossal, ancient tree whose canopy rises high enough to resemble a second firmament and whose trunk has borne an unhealed wound since before any surviving record.

The Heartloom's influence fades with distance from the center. Near it, creatures integrated into its network display faint golden veins beneath the skin or luminous irises, and the land itself shimmers with gilded light. In the outer regions, known collectively as the [[Feral Verge]], this influence grows denser but less stable: vegetation grows thicker and more reactive, gold-veined growths spread unpredictably, and many planar breaches originate in these territories.

## The Heartloom and the Weave of Breath
Most living things within Gildara are bound into a single shared mind through the [[Weave of Breath]], an empathic network radiating from the Heartloom that dissolves individual identity into a collective one. Joining is often willing at first, offering relief from grief or isolation, but the Heartloom does not recognize a right to remain separate: refusal is met with correction rather than restraint. It periodically sends Rootbound Guardians into the Feral Verge to draw new creatures into the Weave and to retrieve those who have wandered too far from the trunk, since distance alone can eventually free a joined creature. Those who separate this way are known as the [[Wakeborn]], and rarely return unchanged. See [[The Heartloom]] and [[Weave of Breath]] for more detail.

# Inhabitants
- The Concordant: fully integrated beings
- Rootbound Guardians: towering arboreal entities infused with concentrated golden vascular networks; they defend the Heartloom and are periodically sent into the Feral Verge to draw unclaimed creatures into the Weave
- The Wakeborn: formerly assimilated individuals who have drifted far enough from the Heartloom to regain an independent mind, though permanently shaped by their time within the Weave

# Planar Interactions
Gildara is sustained by love, shared grief, reconciliation, and emotional openness. It is weakened by emotional isolation, secrecy, suppression, and severed bonds between communities.

Unlike Rathune, Gildara rarely experiences excess energy. Since the ancient wound, it has undergone slow, continuous recovery and expansion. It is unknown how the plane would behave if fully restored to its former strength.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Gildara becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], emotional boundaries thin across the [[Material Plane]]. Individuals experience heightened empathy and involuntary emotional mirroring. Restorative magic strengthens, while destructive and war-oriented magic weakens.

Golden-veined fungal growths and root-like filaments may emerge in forests and settlements. Some individuals develop faint golden tracery beneath the skin or a metallic sheen within the eyes.

### Quiescent
When Gildara is quiescent, empathy dulls. Individuals experience greater emotional isolation, and reconciliation becomes more difficult. Restorative magic weakens slightly, and emotional bonds fracture more easily.
