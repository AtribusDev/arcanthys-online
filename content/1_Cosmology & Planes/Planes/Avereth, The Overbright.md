**Avereth**, often referred to as **the Overbright**, is a [[Planes|plane]] of relentless vitality where decline cannot naturally endure. Suffused with positive energy, Avereth embodies persistence, renewal, and the refusal of life to diminish.

# Description
Avereth is a realm where life and motion never subside. There is no singular sun; light instead floods evenly from everything at once, an unbroken white-hot glare with no source point and no direction to trace it back to. Shadows cannot gather here, not because they are burned away, but because nothing weakens or dims enough to host them.

The landscape itself no longer behaves like plant matter. What passes for ground is thick, fibrous tissue that pulses faintly underfoot; outcrops of bone-white mineral jut upward in overlapping plates, layered like scar over scar rather than shaped by weather. Nothing is cleared away and nothing decays: fallen structures do not rot but calcify, fusing into the growth beneath them, and breaks in the terrain seal over before whatever caused them can drain. Where older accretions crack under the weight of new growth, the fracture heals within hours, thicker and denser than before.

Avereth's intensity is not only physical to the landscape. Those who remain for extended periods exhibit signs of over-saturation themselves: flesh grows dense and fibrous, scars over-heal into ridged plating, bones thicken, and organs enlarge. These growths are not corruption but unchecked reinforcement, pressure building wherever positive energy accumulates faster than a body can disperse it. In advanced cases, the body becomes too saturated to remain stable, splitting along seams of excess vitality as surplus life forces its way outward.

# Planar Interactions
Avereth is fuelled by hope and the refusal to yield, symbolizing the universal drive to persist, overcome limitation, and continue beyond natural bounds.

When Avereth contains an excess of positive energy, it becomes increasingly resonant. As it does, its influence spreads as waves of acceleration rather than light alone. Growth quickens, emotions intensify, and healing energies overflow their intended limits. Planar boundaries strain under the pressure of surplus life.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Avereth becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the [[Material Plane]] brightens. Colours appear more vivid, and wounds mend swiftly. Plant and animal tissue grow at unnatural speeds and can develop large mutations. The effect is strongest at noon, when spells utilising positive energy are empowered.

### Quiescent
When Avereth is quiescent, its grip loosens. Light dims, and a numbness hangs in the air.
