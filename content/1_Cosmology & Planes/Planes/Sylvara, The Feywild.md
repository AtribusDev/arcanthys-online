**Sylvara**, also known as **the Feywild**, is a [[Planes|plane]] of countless stories, in which faerie tales influence both settlements and their inhabitants, and narratives and metaphors shape reality and the laws of physics.

As an [[Inner Planes|inner plane]], Sylvara orbits closer than most other planes, and because of this the layer between planes is comparatively thin. Mortals may cross into Sylvara unintentionally, often without realizing they have done so.

# Description
Sylvara contains imitations of many locales found in [[Arcanthys]], though they appear heightened: more beautiful, more terrible, more meaningful. Forests glow with impossible twilight, rivers sing like distant memory, and castles perch on hills with theatrical inevitability.

The sky remains locked in perpetual dusk or dawn, never advancing to full day or night. Stars slowly reorganize themselves into symbols, archetypes, or constellations significant to those who gaze upon them. The horizon often appears closer than it is, as though the world is framed like a stage.

Geography in Sylvara follows the shape of narrative rather than the shape of the physical world. Mountains rise to isolate cursed kingdoms. Jungles and forests deepen to conceal secrets. Storms gather at moments of betrayal. Biomes that should not coexist may stand side by side if the story demands it.

## Domains
Sylvara is divided into Domains, each devoted to a particular kind of story. Each Domain is governed by an [[Archfey]] who acts both as a ruler and as a Dramaturge, shaping the realm to sustain and escalate the narratives unfolding within it.

## Stories
Stories are not rigid scripts, and inhabitants are not puppets. Rather, Sylvara weaves a subtle pull within the fabric of events, aligning circumstances and accumulating coincidences. Some stories play only once, and others repeat endlessly with subtle variations. Each finished story empowers its respective domain. Many stories turn on trades, trials of wit, and ironic reversals (a bargain honored to the letter but not the spirit, a boast fulfilled too literally, a riddle that unlocks more than a door), since fate in Sylvara favors poetic irony as readily as it favors destiny.

# Planar Interactions
Sylvara is fuelled by joy, curiosity, surprise, and wonder, but perhaps most importantly by longing: the ache of unfinished business, unspoken love, unclaimed crowns, unanswered riddles, and legends that demand completion. Longing here is not only heartbreak; it is the itch of a story that has not yet turned, the pull of a punchline withheld, the need to see how a trick resolves.

When Sylvara has an excess of energy, it becomes increasingly resonant. As it does, its influence spreads: inhabitants might more easily stumble into Sylvara, and fey more actively attempt to lure mortals in.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Sylvara becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the boundary between the planes thins, and passage in either direction can occur easily. The fey lure mortals to their domains, though for reasons of mischief more so than reasons of maliciousness.

During resonant periods, domains, stories, or parts of those can spill into the [[Material Plane]], taking over or merging into settlements, forests, and other biomes. Once the resonant period ends, they fade away as abruptly as they appeared. Enchantment magic is especially potent in these places.

### Quiescent
When Sylvara is quiescent, it becomes almost impossible for mortals or fey to travel between the planes.
