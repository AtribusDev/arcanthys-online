The [[Arcanthys|World of Arcanthys]] spins within the **Material Plane** along with its satellites and [[Arcanum (Sun)]]. The Material Plane is enfolded by the [[Astral Plane]] and the [[Ethereal Plane]]. The [[Cosmology|other planes]] drift within the [[Astral Plane]], and as each draws nearer to the Material Plane, its influence grows stronger.
