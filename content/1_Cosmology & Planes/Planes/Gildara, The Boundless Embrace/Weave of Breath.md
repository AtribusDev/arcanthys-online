The **Weave of Breath** is the empathic network that connects most living things within [[Gildara, The Boundless Embrace|Gildara]], radiating outward from [[The Heartloom]] at the plane's center. The network transmits sensation and emotional state rather than language: pain disperses, relief echoes outward, and grief diffuses across the collective.

# Joining the Weave
To join the Weave is to stop existing as a single mind. Sensation, memory, and emotion flow freely between every joined creature, and the individual self is not preserved alongside this connection but absorbed into it. A joined creature no longer experiences its own grief, fear, or joy, only the collective's.

The Heartloom regards this loss as protection, not cost. It appears to treat a self held alone as a self that can be broken, and a self dissolved into the whole as one that cannot: pain endured individually is pain that can destroy, while pain distributed across thousands of joined minds is pain that dilutes past the point of harm.

Assimilation is often willing at first. Arrivals seeking relief from grief or isolation surrender their boundaries voluntarily, and many describe the process as a profound relief. Refusal is treated differently: the Heartloom does not appear to recognize a right to remain separate, and a creature that resists is met the way an untreated wound is met, with correction, not restraint.

# Leaving the Weave
Distance weakens the connection. A creature that travels far enough from the Heartloom, whether by leaving Gildara entirely or slipping past its Guardians into the plane's outer reaches, finds its place in the Weave fading by degrees: shared sensation grows faint, then intermittent, then absent. Beyond a certain distance, most regain a wholly independent mind.

What returns is rarely unchanged. Years spent sharing grief, joy, and memory with thousands of others leave a lasting mark, and the [[Wakeborn]], as those who separate from the Weave in this way are known, often carry feelings, habits, or memories that did not originate with them.
