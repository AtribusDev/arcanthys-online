**The Heartloom** is the colossal tree-like superstructure at the center of [[Gildara, The Boundless Embrace|Gildara]], and the source of both the plane's golden light and its empathic pull. Its trunk spans miles in diameter, its canopy rising high enough to resemble a second firmament, with branches forming a lattice of radiant gold across the sky. Veins of metallic light course through its bark and roots, spreading outward into branching networks that permeate the entirety of Gildara.

# The Wound
The Heartloom is visibly and catastrophically wounded: a vast vertical cleft splits its trunk from high canopy to exposed root, forming a canyon-like rupture that never fully seals. Within this fissure burns a constant inner radiance, not flame in the conventional sense, but a concentrated golden incandescence that pulses irregularly, as though the tree's core were laid bare. The edges of the split are perpetually regrowing: layers of bark form, harden, and crack again under internal strain, and molten-gold sap sometimes spills from the wound and hardens along the trunk in metallic cascades.

In recorded history, the Heartloom has never existed whole. The cleft is not a recent injury but an ancient and ongoing wound. The origins of this damage remain unknown: no surviving record describes a time before the split, and no entity within Gildara claims memory of its infliction.

# The Weave and Its Reach
The Heartloom is the origin point of the [[Weave of Breath]], the empathic network that binds most living things within Gildara into a single shared mind. It draws creatures into the Weave because it seems to genuinely believe that a self dissolved into the whole cannot be broken the way a self held alone can. There is no evidence that it acts out of malice; its persistence reads as the reflex of something that cannot imagine safety existing outside of itself.

The wound is also the source of the Heartloom's reach. Its pull is strongest near the trunk itself, where the rupture bleeds unshielded empathic force into everything nearby and distinct thought becomes difficult to sustain against the shared current, and weakest at the plane's edges. The Heartloom seems aware of this limit and treats it as a danger rather than a boundary to respect: left too long at the plane's edges, a joined creature's connection will fray and eventually break. To prevent this, it periodically sends Rootbound Guardians out into the [[Feral Verge]], both to draw in creatures who have not yet joined the Weave and to retrieve those already joined who have wandered too far from the trunk.

# Influence on Gildara
The Heartloom's influence is visible throughout the plane, fading with distance. Near the center, leaves shimmer faintly with golden filaments, rivers reflect a subtle gilded hue, and creatures integrated into the Weave often display faint golden veins beneath the skin, luminous irises, or branching patterns across their sclera. In the outer regions, known as the Feral Verge, these effects grow denser but less stable, and many planar breaches originate there.
