**The Afterlife** describes the destinations and processes that govern souls after death. Most follow the [[Cycle of the Soul]], drifting into [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell]] and gradually drawn into [[The Everdeep]], where identity fades as one soul merges into the whole. Not every soul takes this path, however, and a number of exceptions are recognized.

# Spirits
Some souls remain bound to the [[Ethereal Plane]] rather than passing fully into Noctyra. These souls are commonly referred to as [[Spirits|spirits]]. Many are associated with strong attachments to the [[Material Plane]], unresolved purpose, violent death, or places of significant emotional or magical resonance.

Spirits are often tied to particular locations, objects, bloodlines, or events connected to their former lives. In some cases, the resolution or destruction of this connection is said to weaken the spirit or cause it to disappear, though such accounts are inconsistent.

# Claimed Souls
It is also possible for a soul to be claimed by another being before death. Ritual vows, infernal contracts, divine oaths, and certain arcane pacts may bind a soul to an external power, causing it to pass into that entity’s possession rather than entering the Everdeep naturally.

Such claims do not always appear absolute. Some accounts suggest that souls may still be drawn toward the Everdeep unless the claimant exerts sufficient influence to retain them. The fate of claimed souls varies considerably depending on the nature of the being that possesses them.
