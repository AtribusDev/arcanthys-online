**The Cycle of the Soul** describes the commonly accepted passage of souls through life, death, and eventual dissolution. Though details differ between cultures and eras, most surviving traditions agree on its broader structure.

Souls are said to originate from the radiance of [[Arcanum (Sun)|Arcanum]] as it falls upon [[Arcanthys]]. Conscious essence forms within this light and descends into the Material Plane, where it binds itself to a living creature. Over the course of a mortal life, the soul grows through memory, experience, emotion, and force of will, and older souls are often described as carrying greater presence or weight, though the exact nature of this growth remains poorly understood.

# Death and the Everdeep
Upon death, the soul separates from the body and drifts toward [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell]], where it is gradually drawn into [[The Everdeep]], the vast convergence of the dead at the plane's heart. There, the boundaries between souls erode, and identity fades as it merges into the greater whole.

# Resurrection and the Limits of Recovery
This gradual loss of identity sets the practical limits of [[Resurrection|resurrection]]. A soul that has only recently entered the Everdeep is believed to retain enough of itself to be drawn back and restored. The longer it remains, the more thoroughly it dissolves into the convergence, until no amount of skill or power can separate it out again.

Recovery is not without cost to the one attempting it. The Everdeep's pull grows only stronger the deeper it runs, and wresting a single soul free means exerting a counter-pull strong enough to match it. Casters who attempt this are not shielded from what they are fighting against, and accounts describe them left drained, disoriented, or, in the worst cases, briefly caught by the very pull they meant to overcome. The larger the pull required, the greater this risk is said to be.

# What Becomes of a Soul
What ultimately happens to a soul once it has dissolved past the point of recovery is not something common belief agrees on. Some hold that the Everdeep feeds back into Arcanum itself, its spent souls returning to the sun to be given form again and closing the cycle into rebirth. Others maintain that nothing survives the Everdeep at all, that whatever reaches its depths is simply gone, with no further part left to play in the cycle of the soul.
