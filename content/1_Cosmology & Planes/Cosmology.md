**The planar cosmology describes the arrangement of the [[Planes|planes]] of existence around [[Arcanthys]] and the way those planes influence mortal reality.**

# Structure
[[Arcanthys]] exists within the [[Material Plane]], with the [[Astral Plane]] and [[Ethereal Plane]] surrounding it. The nine outer planes are distinct from one another and orbit in relation to the Material Plane.

The outer planes and their emotional fuel are:

| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| [[Sylvara, The Feywild]]              | joy, surprise, curiosity, awe         |
| [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell]]           | sadness, grief, apathy, despair, fear |
| [[Avereth, The Overbright]]           | hope                                  |
| [[Varynth, The Rising Labyrinth]]     | ambition, desire                      |

# Resonance and Emotional Fuel
Each outer plane is sustained by specific emotional or ideological currents expressed by mortal life. When the corresponding currents intensify, the linked plane strengthens and its influence becomes more pronounced; when they diminish, the plane's influence recedes.

Planar proximity affects how strongly a realm can impress its nature upon the Material Plane. Periods of closer alignment are associated with intensified manifestations tied to the approaching plane.

# Demi-Planes
Countless demi-planes exist as dependent realms linked to larger planes. Most remain anchored to a parent plane whose resonance sustains them.

When planar alignments shift, demi-planes can be displaced or absorbed into a different orbit. Such transfers often alter a demi-plane's conditions, inhabitants, and metaphysical stability.

Some minor demi-planes exist wholly within larger planes rather than maintaining an independent orbital path.

# Planar Travel
Travel between planes is possible through established planar methods, principally by accessing the [[Astral Plane]].

The nine outer planes are separate from one another, though travel between them is possible. They are connected by the [[Astral Plane]] but cut off from the [[Ethereal Plane]].
