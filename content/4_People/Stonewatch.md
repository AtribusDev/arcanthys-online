The **Stonewatch** is the collective term for the dwarf clans that still keep an active watch over a [[Wardholds|wardhold]], one of the fortress-holds raised during the [[Age of Shaping]] to guard a sealed prison of [[The Fell Lords|the Fell Lords]].

## Origins

Every clan founded in the split that followed dwarvenkind's judgment of [[Baugrun, The Unsound|Baugrun]] took up a wardhold of its own, built to stand watch over one sealed remnant of the Age of Shaping. Dwarven tradition holds that the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] lent their own hand in raising the wardholds, and saw to it that no clan was ever told which Fell Lord its own wardhold held: what no Stonewatch dwarf knows can never be tricked or tortured out of them, nor bent soft by whatever loyalty a clan might still carry for the elder who once wronged them. In this early period the watch was scattered and hold-by-hold rather than a single coordinated body.

## The Age of the Deep and the Reforging

When the seas rose during the [[Age of the Deep]] and [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru’gol]]'s Drowned Spawn began surfacing in flooded caverns and buried rivers far inland, whole wardholds fell as garrisons were overrun by a Lesser Maw taking root in their lower halls. Dwarven accounts credit their clans as central to the wider mortal effort that shackled the Drowning Deep again at the close of the age, tracking its spawn through the stone and raising the wardwork needed to hold each breach closed. It is this era that dwarven tradition points to as the Stonewatch's true forging into a coordinated, lasting institution rather than a scattered, hold-by-hold vigil.

## Duties and Secrecy

A Stonewatch clan's central duty is guarding its own wardhold and the sealed prison beneath it, a charge kept in near-total silence even from other dwarves. No clan knows which Fell Lord its own wardhold holds, and a clan whose wardhold happens to sit over a particular prison guards even that fact with the same silence it guards everything else about its watch.

## Modern Stonewatch

Most dwarves alive today are not Stonewatch, tracing their clan back to a wardhold lost generations before rather than one still held, and have no active Stonewatch duty in their family's recent memory. Where a clan's watch does continue, it is maintained quietly and without fanfare, its details shared only with those who have earned the clan's full trust. Uru'gol's Drowned Spawn remain part of that inheritance as well, and not only for Stonewatch clans: any dwarven hold that cuts into a flooded seam grown up around a Lesser Maw may seal it, abandon it, or send word to a Stonewatch clan better equipped to deal with it.
