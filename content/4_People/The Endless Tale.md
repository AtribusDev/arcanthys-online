**The Endless Tale** is the accumulated oral record of [[Elves|elven]] history, culture, and legend, added to continuously since the era of the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]]. It exists because so much of what elves once had was broken or erased under Solvarian rule; in response, elves turned the deliberate, communal recitation of memory into a tradition of its own.

## Practice

An addition to the Endless Tale can take many forms: a newly lived event, a legend passed down from before the Dominion, or a fragment of inscription or craft recovered from an elven ruin. Nothing is added until its contents can be confirmed. Once confirmed, it is recited alongside everything already held, becoming a permanent part of the whole rather than a separate record kept apart from it.

## Cultural Weight

Purposefully losing or altering a piece of the Endless Tale is regarded as one of the most serious offenses an elf can commit against their own community. This weight does not come from pride in what has been preserved. It comes from how much elven history is already gone, and from a determination to guard what remains from being lost the same way.
