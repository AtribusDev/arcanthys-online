**Freeborn** is the collective term used across modern-era Arcanthys for peoples other than humans: elves, dwarves, orcs, and the many others once classed as non-human under Solvarian law. The term rose in direct opposition to the language of the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]], which had legally sorted its non-human subjects into "bonded castes" and denied them the rights held by human citizens. To call oneself Freeborn is to state plainly that no such bond ever held, or holds any longer.

## Origins

The word entered common use in the wake of the Dominion's fall, spreading fastest among communities that had lived directly under Solvarian rule. It carries no single point of invention; rather, it appears to have surfaced independently in several liberated regions around the same time; a shared answer to a shared condition, rather than a term coined by any one person, faction, or edict.

## Usage

Freeborn is a political and historical identity, not an ethnic or cultural one. It says nothing about a person's ancestry, homeland, faith, or customs, only that they belong to a people the Dominion once sought to bond rather than govern. Most Freeborn individuals identify first with their own people, nation, or clan, and reach for "Freeborn" mainly in contexts where that shared history is relevant: political speech, treaties, memorials, and dealings with outsiders. It is rarely the first word an elf or an orc would use to describe themselves, but it is one nearly all of them would accept.

Human Solvarites and their descendants are not considered Freeborn, regardless of their present relationship to the Dominion's legacy.

## Historical Usage

Older records and Solvarian-era documents do not use this term; they instead employ the imperial vocabulary of "non-human" and "bonded castes." Wiki pages describing the Solvarian era retain this period-accurate language rather than applying "Freeborn" retroactively, since the term did not exist, and the status it describes did not yet apply, until after the Dominion's fall.
