**Kharox** is a fallen Fell Lord associated with domination, hierarchy, and enforced order, and is linked to the origin of [[Devils]].

Kharox embodied the principle of control through structure. This includes systems of rank, obligation, and authority maintained regardless of moral justification. Unlike [[Vhalis]], whose order is based on law, Kharox represents imposed order: power that sustains itself through enforcement.

This distinction is reflected in the nature of Devils, which operate within strict hierarchies built on contracts, command structures, and coercion.

Kharox is commonly described as the embodiment of _Endless Dominion_.
