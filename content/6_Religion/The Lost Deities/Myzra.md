**Myzra** is a fallen Fell Lord associated with entropy, self-interest, and systemic collapse, and is linked to the origin of [[Yugoloth]].

Myzra embodied the breakdown of structure through individual ambition and competing interests. Rather than direct destruction, this principle manifests as gradual destabilization, where systems fail as their components act independently.

This is reflected in the behavior of [[Yugoloth]], which are typically motivated by self-interest and opportunism. They do not maintain stable hierarchies and frequently shift allegiance when advantageous.

Myzra is commonly described as the embodiment of _Inevitable Entropy_ or _Selfish Dissolution_.
