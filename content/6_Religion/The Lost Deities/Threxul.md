**Threxul** is a Fell Lord associated with destruction, violence, and total collapse, and is linked to the origin of [[Demons]].

Threxul embodied immediate and unrestrained ruin. Unlike [[Myzra]], which represents gradual decay, Threxul represents direct annihilation: systems ending through force rather than erosion.

This principle is reflected in the nature of [[Demons]], which are characterized by impulsive behavior, aggression, and a lack of sustained structure. Their actions typically result in rapid and widespread destruction.

Threxul is commonly described as the embodiment of _Ruin and Devastation_.
