The **Lost Deities** are four gods believed to have died during the later stages of the [[The War of Shaping|War of Shaping]], in an event centered on [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]] and [[The Everdeep]]. The Lost Deities include:
- [[The Fell Lords]] [[Kharox]], [[Myzra]] and [[Threxul]].
- The [[Gods|God]] [[Vhalis]].

They are the only known deities to have perished without return, and are widely regarded as the origin of both [[Celestials]] and [[Fiends]].
