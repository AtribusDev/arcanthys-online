**Vhalis** is the fallen god of perfect order and judgment, associated with [[Celestials]].

Vhalis embodied absolute law, impartial judgment, and the enforcement of cosmic balance. Unlike deities concerned with mercy or compassion, Vhalis is consistently described as adhering to a strict and inflexible system of rules. These laws applied universally, including to Vhalis itself.

This constraint is a defining aspect of the deity. Several accounts note that Vhalis did not act freely, but only within the limits of an established cosmic order.

Following its destruction in [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]], the essence of Vhalis is believed to have given rise to [[Celestials]]. These beings reflect its nature through rigid hierarchies, defined purpose, and a structured interpretation of morality.
