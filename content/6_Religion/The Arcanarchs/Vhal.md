**Vhal** is the Arcanarch of Protection, Guardians, and Vigilance. He is both protector and enforcer, patron of guards, bodyguards, sentries, watchmen, wardens, and all who stand between danger and the vulnerable.

He embodies the spirit of watchfulness itself, whether used for noble or selfish purposes. To him, protection is not an act of kindness but a duty. A wall exists to hold. A guard exists to watch. A protector exists to protect.

Yet he teaches that excessive protection breeds weakness. Those under a shield must never forget how to survive without one.

# Description
Vhal is often depicted as a tall armored figure whose face is hidden behind a visor engraved with a single unblinking eye. His armor bears no ornamentation beyond countless marks and dents, each representing a duty fulfilled.

In some depictions he carries a shield and spear, while in others he holds a lantern whose light reveals hidden threats. His gaze is said to pierce darkness, deception, and complacency alike.

His holy symbol is an open eye set within a shield.

# Personality
Vhal is vigilant, disciplined, and practical.

Unlike many gods associated with protection, he is not motivated by compassion. He protects because protection is necessary for civilization to endure.

He despises negligence above nearly all other faults. A guard who abandons their post earns more of his wrath than a thief who exploits the opportunity.

He values preparation over bravery, caution over glory, and duty over sentiment.

The Arcanarch believes that danger can never be completely eliminated. People who expect perfect safety become complacent and eventually create greater disasters through their carelessness.

He is capable of mercy, but rarely offers comfort. His lessons are often harsh and uncomfortable.

His most common saying is:
_"A shield is a promise, not a miracle."_

# Powers
Vhal grants his followers the ability to enhance their protective and defensive capabilities. Those who have earned his favor may experience:

- **Heightened Vigilance**: Increased awareness of surroundings and ability to detect threats, deception, and hidden dangers
- **Armored Resolve**: Blessings upon armor and shields, bolstering their durability and protective power
- **Watchful Eyes**: Ability to see in darkness and pierce through illusions and magical deception
- **Steadfast Guardianship**: Enhanced endurance and focus when protecting others or maintaining posts
- **Boundary Blessing**: Power to ward locations and fortify defenses against intrusion

# Worshippers
Followers of Vhal are seen as vigilant, disciplined, and often severe. They are practical protectors without sentimentality, and tend to attract pragmatists who understand protection as duty rather than charity.

**Primary worship communities**:
- The [[Wardens' Guild]] and [[Shieldbearers' Guild]] operate through the [[Vigil Covenant]]
- Military units and city watch organizations
- Professional bodyguards and escort services
- Sentries, watchmen, and guards of all kinds

**Core tenets of worship**:
- Protect others according to contract and duty: no more, no less
- Others must learn to protect themselves through whatever means available (strength, coin, wit, etc.)
- Those who refuse self-sufficiency are not worth the effort to protect
- Negligence in duty is a sin greater than any crime
- Constant vigilance against complacency is required


## Rites of Vhal
Vhal does not demand grand rituals or ostentatious prayer. He values action over words.

- **Through vigilance**: Standing watch with focus and purpose, maintaining awareness even in monotony
- **Through duty**: Fulfilling protective responsibilities with discipline and without complaint
- **Through prayer**: Quiet invocations offered while performing protective duties, acknowledging the weight of the responsibility
- **Through preparation**: Maintaining readiness, training, and fortifying defenses
- **Through tough love**: Teaching others to stand on their own, removing safety nets when they have grown dependent
