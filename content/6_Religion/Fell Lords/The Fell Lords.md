The **Fell Lords** are regarded as the strongest of fiends, each embodying a mortal fear that it uses to corrupt the world. The Fell Lords are demi-gods and, so far, have proven immortal. Legends hold that they ruled parts of [[Arcanthys]] during [[Age of Shaping|The Age of Shaping]].

A Fell Lord can spawn fiends or other creatures but most fiends are believed to be related to the Fell Lords of [[The Lost Deities]].
