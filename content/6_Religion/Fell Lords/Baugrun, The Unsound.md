**Baugrun**, called the Unsound, is one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], an immortal fiendish demi-god. According to legend, it shaped the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] during the [[Age of Shaping]]. It embodies the fear that sits beneath every dwarf's trust in stone and craft: that what has been tested, trusted, and built upon without doubt can still give way without warning, whether swallowed slowly by a passage giving out beneath it or brought down all at once when the ground itself starts to shake, burying whoever stood on it.

# Description

Baugrun is depicted as a dwarf grown to impossible size, a colossal figure of stone and ore, broad through the shoulders and bent low, as though still braced under some old weight. Its beard is a slope of solid stone reaching past where its knees would be, grooved as though carved.

Deep fault-lines run across its shoulders, chest, and beard, wide enough to see into and lit from within by a dull red glow, as though something molten sits just beneath the stone. The cracks do not stay fixed: plates of rock along their edges grind slowly against one another, shedding loose scree that gathers in drifts at its feet. Baugrun's eyes are two of these same fissures set into its face, thin seams of red light in place of any recognizable eye.

# Personality

Dwarves are known for grudges kept across lifetimes, but even by that measure, Baugrun's is treated as something apart. A dwarf's grudge can, in time, be judged and closed. Baugrun's grudges are never closed, no matter how many centuries pass.

Baugrun also has a deep greed for diamonds, gemstones, and other valuables drawn from the depths, and that greed is said to be the surest way to deal with it. Its favor can be bargained for with the right tribute. But the same greed cuts both ways: a debt to Baugrun left open too long is dangerous. What starts as an unpaid due does not stay forgotten; left long enough, it hardens into a grudge, and Baugrun is said to settle a grudge directly, with an earthquake that swallows whatever it judges itself still owed, ore, treasure, or the miners themselves.

# Powers

# Worshippers

Baugrun's worship is confined almost entirely to evil-aligned peoples of the [[Underdark]]. [[Duergar]] hold its particular favor. Duergar mining clans name Baugrun before opening a new seam and set aside the first stone or ore drawn from any working as tribute.

# Relations

Baugrun confines with many other Fell Lords. Trading services and favors for fast riches.

# History

## Age of Shaping

### Baugrun's Shaping and the First Grudge

Legends say Baugrun shaped the first dwarves during the [[Age of Shaping]], stripping them whole from its own body of living stone. For a time afterward, it served as dwarvenkind's first and only elder, teaching the earliest clans to read stone, to build to outlast themselves, and to demand proof before trust, the same standard dwarves still hold each other to today.

According to the same accounts, when war broke out between the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], Baugrun stood with the First Gods and set its own people to driving new seams deep into the earth for the ore and stone the war demanded. It was in that digging that dwarvenkind cut into the [[Worldcore]], something no dwarf had found before; some traditions call it a stone unlike any other, others a seam without end. Whatever it was, it took hold of Baugrun as nothing before it had, and its want for the find hardened quickly.

The legends go on to say that when Baugrun's own people confronted it over the change, Baugrun turned on them, and declared that dwarvenkind owed it service for everything it had shaped and taught them. It broke with the First Gods that day, refusing to yield to any dwarf's judgment, and threw its lot in with the Fell Lords. Dwarvenkind refused Baugrun and held to the First Gods through to the war's end: dwarvenkind's first and oldest grudge, shared by all dwarves at once from that moment on.
