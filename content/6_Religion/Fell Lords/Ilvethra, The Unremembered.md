**Ilvethra**, called the Unremembered, is one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]]: an immortal fiendish demi-god credited with shaping the elves during the [[Age of Shaping]]. It embodies the fear of being forgotten, of a life, a name, or a deed leaving no trace and having therefore counted for nothing.

# Description

Ilvethra takes the shape of an elf, tall and slender, utterly without gender and without a face: smooth, featureless An elf-shape with no face to recognize and no voice of its own, heard only through the mouths and memories of others.

# Personality

Ilvethra does not seek worship so much as certainty that it will never be forgotten. Its particular cruelty lies in what it leaves behind when it takes a memory. Rather than an absence, it inserts itself into the gap: the forgotten person is not simply erased but quietly replaced by a warped recollection of Ilvethra having stood in their place, a parent, a friend, a fallen companion, remembered as bearing some resemblance to it even as every genuine detail of the original is lost. It regards this as preservation, not deletion. A world that forgets its dead but always remembers something of Ilvethra is, by its own accounting, a fair exchange.

# Powers

Ilvethra's dominion is chiefly one of memory, substitution, and record. It is credited with:

- **The Standing-In**: rather than leaving a blank space where a memory is taken, Ilvethra fills it with a distorted recollection of itself performing that role. The substitute is never a clean fit: something about the face, the voice, or the manner remains subtly, unaccountably wrong, though the victim rarely notices while it is happening
- **The Nameless**: those whose every memory has been fully claimed this way, remembered nowhere, by no one, as themselves, persist as hollow, purposeless thralls, their remaining existence borrowed from Ilvethra's presence in the memories that replaced them
- **[[Doppelgangers]]**: even while bound, a fraction of Ilvethra's power can slip free in this form, beings capable of assuming another's face and life in the flesh where Ilvethra itself, sealed as it is, can only act through memory
- **The Ledger Unseen**: an apparent awareness of who still remembers it truly, who has forgotten it, and who now unknowingly carries a memory of it standing in someone else's place
- **Dominion of Record**: influence over inscriptions, epitaphs, and oral history, subtly altering what is written or recited to include some trace of itself

# Worshippers

Ilvethra draws worship from two kinds of people. The first fear their own insignificance more than they fear death, and its cults, small and scattered, offer commemoration and ritual repetition of its name and deeds in exchange for a promise that the worshipper, too, will never be forgotten. The second have survived a trauma severe enough that they wish to forget it entirely, and come to Ilvethra for its particular gift: not a blank space, but a replacement, understanding, at least in part, that whatever fills the gap it leaves behind will not be the truth.

# Relations

Elves hold no active connection to Ilvethra beyond an old, inherited dread of exactly this sort of faceless, memory-hungry thing, rarely explained and rarely questioned. What history survives of [[The Borrowed Faces]] suggests that dread is well founded: elves were the first target of the [[Doppelgangers]] Ilvethra spawned while bound. Among the other Fell Lords, Ilvethra keeps no known allies.

# History

Elven tradition traces the origin of elves to the [[Age of Shaping]], when Ilvethra is credited with giving them form. Having witnessed the wonder of Sylvara's endless stories, it is said to have wished for a measure of that same wonder to exist within its own creation, and so shaped elves with a natural affinity for the [[Sylvara, The Feywild|Feywild]] from the beginning, a bond that lingers in elven bloodlines to this day. Like the other Fell Lords, it sought to bind its creation permanently to itself, a stance that put it at odds with the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and contributed to the war that closed out the age; Ilvethra was bound within [[Arcanthys]] when that war ended, sealed rather than destroyed.

## The First Incursion: The Borrowed Faces
Ilvethra has had a single know incursion since it was sealed. The first sign that Ilvethra's seals had weakened came in the event now known as [[The Borrowed Faces]]: a wave of quiet substitutions in which the first [[Doppelgangers]] ever recorded, formed from a fraction of Ilvethra's own power, infiltrated elven communities and replaced loved ones in their place. From unions formed during this period, the first [[Changelings]] were born.

The plot was uncovered before it could spread further, and Ilvethra's seals were strengthened specifically to sever whatever hold it might otherwise have claimed over the changelings born of it. See [[The Borrowed Faces]] for the fuller account.
