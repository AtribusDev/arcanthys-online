**Ilvethra**, called the Unremembered, is one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], an immortal fiendish demi-god. Legends say it shaped the [[Elves|elves]] during the [[Age of Shaping]]. It embodies the fear of being forgotten, of a life, a name, or a deed leaving no trace and having therefore counted for nothing.

# Description

Ilvethra is depicted as an elf, tall and slender, utterly without gender and without a face: smooth and featureless, an elf-shape with no face to recognize and no voice of its own, heard only through the mouths and memories of others.

# Personality

Ilvethra offers a gentle service to those who have endured more grief than they can carry, removing a memory of sorrow entirely so the sufferer no longer carries its weight. Its cruelty lies in what fills the absence that memory leaves behind. In its place, Ilvethra inserts a memory of its own making: a parent, a friend, or a fallen companion, recast with a warmth the original may never have carried. The face in this substitute is wrong, the voice unfamiliar, and every detail beyond that warmth blurred past recognition, yet the recollection insists some part of Ilvethra was present in the original person all along. What is left afterward is a fondness the mourner still feels for someone who no longer has any features of their own.

Ilvethra performs this exchange for its own sake as much as for the mourner's: it wants to be remembered and loved, however faintly and under however borrowed a face, and every warmth it leaves behind is a small claim staked on someone else's memory. That desire is sharpest toward elves, the people legends say it shaped during the [[Age of Shaping]], and it has been known to act on it directly rather than wait for elves to come to it grieving on their own.

# Worshippers

Ilvethra primarily draws worship from two kinds of people: those who want to forget something themselves, and those who want something forgotten by everyone else who still remembers it.

The first have survived a trauma severe enough that they wish to forget it entirely, and come to Ilvethra for its particular gift, a fabricated replacement that fills the gap where the memory used to be, understanding, at least in part, that whatever fills that gap will not be the truth. The second carry a secret they need no one else to remember, and come to Ilvethra asking the same gift for whoever else holds it: that person's memory emptied of the secret and filled instead with something that never happened.

# Relations

Elves hold no active connection to Ilvethra beyond an old, inherited dread of exactly this sort of faceless, memory-hungry thing, rarely explained and rarely questioned. What history survives of [[The Borrowed Faces]] suggests that dread is well founded: elves were the first target of the [[Doppelgangers]] Ilvethra spawned while bound. Among the other Fell Lords, Ilvethra keeps no known allies.

# History

According to myth, elves originated during the [[Age of Shaping]], when Ilvethra gave them form. Having witnessed the wonder of Sylvara's endless stories, it is said to have wished for a measure of that same wonder to exist within its own creation, and so shaped elves with a natural affinity for the [[Sylvara, The Feywild|Feywild]] from the beginning, a bond that lingers in elven bloodlines to this day. Like the other Fell Lords, it sought to bind its creation permanently to itself, a stance that put it at odds with the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and contributed to the war that closed out the age; Ilvethra was bound within [[Arcanthys]] when that war ended, sealed rather than destroyed.

## The First Incursion: The Borrowed Faces
Ilvethra has had a single known incursion since it was sealed. The first sign that Ilvethra's seals had weakened came in the event now known as [[The Borrowed Faces]]: a wave of quiet substitutions in which the first [[Doppelgangers]] ever recorded, formed from a fraction of Ilvethra's own power, infiltrated elven communities and replaced loved ones in their place. From unions formed during this period, the first [[Changelings]] were born.

The plot was uncovered before it could spread further, and Ilvethra's seals were strengthened specifically to sever whatever hold it might otherwise have claimed over the changelings born of it. See [[The Borrowed Faces]] for the fuller account.
