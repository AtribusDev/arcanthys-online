**Kaldrus**, called the Chain Eternal, is one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]]: an immortal fiendish demi-god whose entire dominion concerns slavery and the loss of freedom. According to legend, Kaldrus shaped no people of its own during the [[Age of Shaping]] and instead enslaved many others.

# Description

Kaldrus is depicted as a great humanoid figure. Above his shoulders sit two maned lion's heads, both wearing a crown made of thick interlocking rings. At the center of his chest sits a dark hole, chain spilling from it in place of blood, each length running outward to a captive. Most depictions of Kaldrus have him seated on a throne made of human slaves, sitting proud.

# Personality

Kaldrus takes open pleasure in another's subjugation and in control itself. He finds far greater satisfaction in a will forced to yield against its own wishes than in one that never resisted at all. A captive already inclined to obey holds little interest for him. What he wants is the moment a mind still fighting him gives way regardless.

That preference makes him patient in a particular way. He is willing to let defiance run for years before breaking it, since a captive worn down slowly yields him more satisfaction than one broken in a single stroke, and he pays as much attention to the manner of a subject's submission, whether it comes with pleading, cursing, or silence, as to the submission itself.

# Worshippers

Those with grand ambitions to rule or command others often seek Kaldrus's favor, drawn to bending others to their purpose and rarely content with the station they already hold. Kaldrus is almost never worshipped openly, and many who keep faith with him go unnoticed even by those closest to them.

Kaldrus's worshippers can bring real benefit to those around them. A settlement whose leader prays to him may prosper under a firmer, more decisive hand than it otherwise would, though what arrangements produce that success are rarely brought into the open, and no one outside the leader's closest circle can say for certain what they cost.

Kaldrus's clearest following is the [[Cult of the Chain]], whose own membership is disproportionately [[Bloodlings|bloodling]] halfling, drawn from a people with little history of grasping after rank.

# History

## Age of Shaping

Kaldrus spent the age enslaving and bending other peoples to its will, folk it never claimed or named as its own, taken only for what obedience could be wrung from them. It went into the war, and into its binding, having built nothing of its own, only whatever submission it could hold onto for as long as it could hold it.
