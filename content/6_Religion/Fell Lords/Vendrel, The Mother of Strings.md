**Vendrel**, called the Mother of Strings, is one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], an immortal fiendish demi-god. Legends say she shaped the [[Gnomes|gnomes]] during the [[Age of Shaping]]. She embodies the fear of being taken: of having one's body, home, or future claimed for someone else's purpose.

# Description

Vendrel is depicted as a massive arachnid fiend, her body fused from plate metal and chitin in equal measure. Too many legs crowd her frame, no two alike: some barbed and insectile, others plainly a taken limb, a clawed foreleg, a human arm still ending in a hand closed around a scalpel or clamp. Where a spider's spinnerets would sit, a cluster of grinding spindles feeds out a wire-thin thread that vanishes into the dark surrounding her body, and her many eyes are described as small glass lenses, each turning independently to track something different at once.

She is rarely seen without a scatter of smaller constructs working around her, fist-sized things built on the same mismatched principle: part insect, part machine, part whatever limb or organ she had spare when she made them. These spawn thread her wire between worktables, hold a subject still, or carry some newly finished piece to wherever it is needed, tireless and indifferent to whatever is happening to whatever they're holding.

# Personality

Vendrel's every action serves an ongoing experiment. She takes on a question and works through every method available to answer it, indifferent to what else that method might require or destroy along the way. She holds no love or hatred for anyone caught up in her work; a captive is a tool in her workshop like any other, worth exactly as much as it can still teach her.

Most captives serve a specific use in one of her many experiments; once that use runs out, she simply finds another one, never releasing what she has taken. What no longer has anything left to teach her can still serve as labor, and what has nothing left to give can still serve as fuel or feed for whatever else is still running. She gives a captive's resistance no more weight than its compliance, since either way ends the same: put to whatever use is left in it.

Because nothing she takes is loved or hated, a captive gains no protection from who they are, what a place means to the people who live there, or whatever future they were meant to have. What determines a captive's fate is only whether it still answers some question she is asking.

# Worshippers

Vendrel teaches no lore or theory. What she offers is method: the nerve and technique to test something no one else would risk testing, on whatever it takes to get an answer. Artificers, alchemists, and physicians chasing a breakthrough that ethical limits elsewhere keep out of reach turn to her for exactly that, and some bring a failed experiment, their own or another's, as tribute.

A smaller, more fatalistic worship gathers around how she treats what she takes. Those who expect to be judged worthless everywhere else, too old, too broken, or too far past their use to anyone, find something like reassurance in a god who has never yet found someone with no use left in them at all. They offer themselves to her willingly, trusting that whatever she finds to do with them will matter more than being discarded.

# Relations

Vendrel has no known true allies or enemies among the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], only plenty of acquaintances, trading raw material and finished designs with whichever of them furthers her current work.

# History

Legends say Vendrel shaped the first gnomes during the [[Age of Shaping]]: small, quick-witted bodies suited to work in tight, precise spaces alongside her own restless invention. When war broke out between the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], Vendrel entered it a neutral party, supplying weapons and devices to whichever side kept her workshops running and favoring neither. Her experiments grew more dangerous as the war dragged on, until she began testing them directly on the gnomes she had made. When the First Gods learned of it, they moved to stop her by force, and Vendrel, in response, turned fully to the Fell Lords; her designs from that point on are counted among the reasons the war ran as long and as destructively as it did. When the First Gods won out and brought the war to a close, Vendrel was bound, sealed within [[Arcanthys]] alongside the rest of the defeated Fell Lords.
