**Fiends** are beings born of malevolent essence within the [[Outer Planes]]. They stand in stark contrast to [[Celestials]], embodying corruption, selfishness, and destructive desire.

A fiend may, on rare occasion, perform an act that appears benevolent, but such deeds are never altruistic: every action ultimately serves its own ambition, whether that be power, influence, or the suffering of others.

Fiends are broadly divided into three primary categories:

| Type         | Alignment    | Description                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Devils]]   | Lawful Evil  | Tyrants of structure, bound by hierarchy, contracts, and calculated control. |
| [[Yugoloth]] | Neutral Evil | Mercenary embodiments of selfishness, driven by opportunity above all.       |
| [[Demons]]   | Chaotic Evil | Manifestations of unrestrained destruction, reveling in chaos and suffering. |
 
Fiends rarely stray far from their native planes, where their power is greatest. Beyond these realms, their forms weaken, most notably under the light of [[The Sun|the Sun]], whose radiance disrupts their essence.

When a fiend is slain on [[Arcanthys]], its destruction is not final. Some return to their home plane, but many are drawn instead to [[Rathune, The Crimson Expanse]], where they are reborn into the Eternal War, bound to endless conflict. Despite this weakening, fiends continue to seek out the Material Plane, drawn by its souls, its struggles, and its potential for corruption.

# Origin

Fiends are generally believed to have originated during the later stages of the [[The War of Shaping|War of Shaping]], when the divine conflict extended into [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]].

During this period, three of the [[The Fell Lords]] are said to have attempted to manipulate [[The Everdeep|the Everdeep]]. A fourth deity, said to be [[Vhalis]], intervened. Outnumbered, Vhalis drew the three Fell Lords into the depths of [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]]. All four deities were subsequently destroyed, and Celestials and Fiends began to appear across the planes, formed from the remains of the fallen gods and altered by the Everdeep. Celestials are said to share their origin with Vhalis, while each type of fiend is tied to one of the fallen Fell Lords:
- [[Kharox]] for Devils
- [[Myzra]] for Yugoloths
- [[Threxul]] for Demons
