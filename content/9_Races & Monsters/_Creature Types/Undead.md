**Undead** are creatures that have died and have then been animated or reborn by supernatural means. Unlike the living, undead exist in defiance of the natural [[Cycle of the Soul]].

Most undead possess a strong connection to [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]], having been infused with its essence. This energy does not merely animate the corpse or spirit, but actively suppresses the natural processes of decay, rest, and spiritual dissolution. As a result, undead are sustained not by life, but by the absence and negation of it.

Undead created through especially powerful or deliberate means may reclaim their souls from the [[The Everdeep]]. However, this reclamation is almost never without consequence. The soul is typically warped or scarred by prolonged exposure to Negative Energy that sustains it, resulting in profound changes to the creature’s morality, emotions, and perception of the world. In most cases, this process drives the undead toward an inherently evil nature.

Undead have an irrepressible instinct to hunt the living. This impulse is a fundamental consequence of their bond to [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]], which reacts violently to the presence of life and vital essence. For many undead, this manifests as hunger, aggression, or fixation, compelling them to seek out living creatures even in the absence of conscious intent or malice.

Despite their transformation, undead may retain lingering instincts, skills, and habits from life. These remnants are not true continuations of identity, but rather imprinted behaviours carried over into undeath. For example, a former soldier may instinctively adopt a defensive stance.
