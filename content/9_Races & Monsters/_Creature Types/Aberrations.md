**Aberrations** are creatures whose bodies and minds follow no law native to Arcanthys, entities that look and think like nothing the Material Plane produced on its own. The term covers aboleths, beholders, mind flayers, and the countless lesser horrors that share their unnatural bearing, and it names a shared nature rather than a single ancestry.

# Origins

No single origin accounts for aberrations as a whole. The type describes what a creature is, not where it came from, and at least three distinct sources are recognized.

The oldest and best-documented traces back to the [[Kelthrae]], the alien aberrations who reshaped much of Arcanthys during the [[Age of the Baneful]]. Facing organized resistance, the Kelthrae bred the first aboleths, beholders, and mind flayers as servants built for war, and descendants of those creations remain the aberrations most commonly encountered in Arcanthys today.

Others answer to no maker at all. Aberrations are said to spawn unbidden within the [[Astral Plane]], drawn into being out of the concentrated emotional energy that drifts there from across the planes, with no more purpose behind their existence than any other creature born to survive.

A newer source has emerged since [[The Second Dawn]]. Regions consumed by severe outbreaks of [[Mageblight]] give rise to roaming aberrations born directly from the corruption itself, warped into being by unstable lumir rather than any deliberate hand.

# Behavior

Regardless of origin, aberrations are marked by a near-universal hostility toward every other form of life, tempered by an equally consistent tolerance for one another. An aberration encountered alone is typically driven by immediate need, hunger, territory, or simple survival, rather than any larger design, and its behavior rarely extends beyond the moment of the encounter.

# Aberrations and the Kelthrae

Every Kelthrae is an aberration, but the reverse is not true. What sets a Kelthrae apart from an aberration spawned in the Astral Plane or born of a Mageblight outbreak is intent: a Kelthrae's actions, however unreadable, plainly serve something beyond mere survival, while most other aberrations pursue nothing more than their own continuation.

The Kelthrae also seem to have a particular knack for drawing other aberrations into their fold, regardless of what spawned them. Aberrations found operating alongside a Kelthrae, in numbers or coordination beyond what chance would explain, are generally assumed to have been gathered rather than born that way. This is part of why an encounter involving a Kelthrae is treated so differently from an ordinary aberration sighting: where one is a hazard to be avoided or put down, the other may be evidence of something with an actual plan.
