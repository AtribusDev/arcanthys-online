**Celestials** are beings born of benevolent essence within the [[Outer Planes]]. They stand in stark contrast to [[Fiends]], embodying purity, selflessness, and justice.

To an outside observer, a celestial's actions or inaction may appear malevolent; to the celestial itself, these acts are undertaken in pursuit of justice. Most celestials are [[Angels]], powerful beings bound by strict laws that rarely permit them to act beyond their own realms, though any creature born of, or heavily shaped by, positive energy may be classified as a celestial.

Celestials rarely stray far from their native planes, where their power is greatest. Beyond these realms, their forms weaken, most notably under the light of [[The Sun|the Sun]], whose radiance disrupts their essence.

When a celestial is slain on [[Arcanthys]], its destruction is not final. Some return to their home plane, but many are drawn instead to [[Rathune, The Crimson Expanse]], where they are reborn into the Eternal War, bound to endless conflict. Despite this weakening, their commitment to good remains strong enough to draw them to the [[Material Plane]].

# Origin

Celestials are generally believed to have originated during the later stages of the [[The War of Shaping|War of Shaping]], when the divine conflict extended into [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]].

During this period, three of the [[The Fell Lords]] are said to have attempted to manipulate [[The Everdeep|the Everdeep]]. A fourth deity, said to be [[Vhalis]], intervened. Outnumbered, Vhalis drew the three Fell Lords into the depths of [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell|Noctyra]]. All four deities were subsequently destroyed, and Celestials and Fiends began to appear across the planes, formed from the remains of the fallen gods and altered by the Everdeep. Celestials are said to share their origin with Vhalis, while each type of fiend is tied to one of the fallen Fell Lords:
- [[Kharox]] for Devils
- [[Myzra]] for Yugoloths
- [[Threxul]] for Demons
