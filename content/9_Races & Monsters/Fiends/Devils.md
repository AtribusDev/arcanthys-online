**Devils** are a type of [[Outsider|outsider]]: fiends that embody law and evil. Devils are said to have originated from the death of [[Kharox]]. They are most often found on the [[Outer Planes]] and weaken when they venture onto the [[Material Plane]].

Devils, like most fiends, are immortal but not invincible. To permanently destroy a devil, it must be slain on its home plane; defeating it on the Material Plane only drives it back to its home plane to reform, or draws it into the Eternal War of [[Rathune, The Crimson Expanse|Rathune]].
