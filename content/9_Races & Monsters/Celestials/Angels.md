**Angels** are the most prominent of [[Celestials]]. They are creatures with a strong sense of justice, law, and selflessness, though their strict codes often forbid them from interfering in events beyond their own realms. When an angel's sense of justice outweighs its sense of law, it may choose exile: stripped of its wings and cast down to the [[Material Plane]], unshackled from the chains of law.

Angels are native to the [[Outer Planes]], where their power is greatest. When slain on [[Arcanthys]], many are drawn to [[Rathune, The Crimson Expanse]], where they are reborn into the Eternal War.
