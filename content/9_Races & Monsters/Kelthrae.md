**The Kelthrae** are the alien [[Aberrations|aberrations]] that spread across [[Arcanthys]] during the [[Age of the Baneful]], twisting the land and the life upon it into shapes nothing in the natural world had produced before. Where most aberrations answer to nothing beyond their own survival, every Kelthrae's actions point toward some larger design, and neither where they came from nor what that design might be is something any scholar of the modern age can say with confidence.

# Origin

The Kelthrae are first recorded in the wake of the gods' withdrawal from the Material Plane at the close of the [[Age of Shaping]]. Where they came from is disputed.

Some hold the Kelthrae as a lingering aftereffect of the Age of Shaping's own upheavals, something the world produced out of itself once the gods stopped watching. Others say they came from somewhere beyond the known planes entirely, arriving in Arcanthys as its first visible mark. A third account holds that the Kelthrae are older still, that they followed the Fell Lords when the Fell Lords first came to Arcanthys from beyond, riding in unseen behind them, and lay dormant and unnoticed for the rest of the Age of Shaping until the gods' withdrawal finally let them stir. A fourth holds that the Kelthrae were never anything apart from the gods at all, but unfinished creation left behind by one divine faction or the other, shaped partway and then abandoned before it could be given true form or purpose. No one knows for certain, and the truth of it is not something the mortals of Arcanthys have ever settled.

What is agreed upon is that nothing recognizable followed in the Kelthrae's wake. They raised no banners, held no ground, and claimed no subjects. Wherever they settled, the world itself simply stopped behaving as it had before.

# Nature and Methods

The Kelthrae did not conquer the lands they touched so much as unmake them. Rivers ran the wrong color, forests grew teeth, and stone remembered shapes it had never held before. The plants, beasts, and other life the gods had made were folded into something else entirely under the Kelthrae's attention, and from this remaking came the first [[Elementals]], [[Oozes]], [[Undead]], and [[Intelligent Plants]].

The Kelthrae themselves are described as bearing no true likeness to anything born of Arcanthys: shifting geometries, too many or too few limbs, colors that do not otherwise occur in nature, and a bearing that unsettles animals and mortals alike long before any threat becomes apparent. They are also said to move more easily through the [[Astral Plane]] than through the Material Plane, and are encountered there, and in places where the boundary between the two runs thin, far more often than chance alone would explain. Ruins, rifts, and stretches of land still scarred from the Age of the Baneful are among the likeliest places to find one stirring even now.

Nothing about their behavior suggests any single plan. Each Kelthrae pursues its own transformations on its own terms, with no apparent coordination with the others, which is a large part of why their intentions remain so hard to read.

What coordination they do command tends to come from elsewhere. Kelthrae have a marked knack for drawing other [[Aberrations|aberrations]] into their fold, regardless of what spawned those aberrations in the first place, so that a pocket of aberrations moving with more purpose than usual is often taken as a sign of a Kelthrae's presence before any physical trace of one is found.

# The Baneful Resistance

The scale of the unmaking eventually provoked an organized response, remembered by later ages as the Baneful Resistance rather than a war in the ordinary sense: it was the Kelthrae's spread that forced mortal and god-touched alike to act, not any ambition of their own. The [[True Dragons|dragons]] entered into a pact with the spawn of the Fell Lord [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]], and this agreement in turn brought the dragons into alliance with the [[Krakens]], despite the deep differences between the two peoples. Both regarded the Kelthrae as a threat that could not simply be waited out.

The resistance that followed was long, and the Kelthrae, formidable as each one was alone, had no shared strategy to fall back on when faced with allies who did. In answer, they began breeding servants built for war rather than for reshaping: the first aboleths, beholders, and mind flayers among them, alongside other aberrations. These creations fought with a coordination their makers had never managed themselves, and their appearance marked the turn of the conflict.

## Defeat, Sealing, and Banishment

The weakest of the Kelthrae fell to the Resistance outright. The most powerful could not be killed by any means their opponents possessed, and what became of them afterward is remembered differently depending on who tells it. Some traditions hold that these survivors were sealed in place, bound by rites drawing on knowledge older than the Age of the Baneful itself, and left hidden somewhere within Arcanthys. Others say the mightiest were not sealed at all but banished outright, hurled beyond the Material Plane into the outer reaches of the [[Astral Plane]], further than any god or mortal commonly travels. Which fate met which Kelthrae, or whether both were used side by side, no surviving record says clearly.

## Aftermath

Not everything the Kelthrae built for the war died with them. Cut loose from whatever had guided them, their creations endured on their own, and spread far beyond wherever the Kelthrae had first set them loose upon the world.

# Modern Understanding

Those same creations are, by a wide margin, the most common aberrations encountered in Arcanthys today. An adventurer who crosses an aboleth, a beholder, or a mind flayer is meeting Kelthrae spawn, whether or not they know it.

The Kelthrae themselves are not believed extinct, only quiet. Neither their seals nor their banishments are thought to be permanent, and it is understood that whatever holds the surviving Kelthrae will eventually give way. When that will happen, and which of them it frees first, no one currently living can say, though given how readily the Kelthrae already seem to reach the Astral Plane, it is those reaches, and the places closest to them, that many expect to feel it first.
