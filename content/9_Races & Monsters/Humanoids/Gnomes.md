**Gnomes** are a humanoid ([[Freeborn]]) people of Arcanthys, recognized across the world for their skill at invention and for a temperament that resists being told what to do. Their history under the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]] shapes gnomish culture more than anything inherited from before it, scattering them into a diaspora that never fully settled again.

## Physiology

Gnomes stand small, rarely taller than three and a half feet, with skin in earthy tans and browns and eyes disproportionately large and bright, often in colors uncommon among humans. Ears and noses run larger than a human's in proportion to the rest of the face, and hair grows thick, wild, and prone to unusual colors and textures that many gnomes groom into deliberately elaborate shapes.

## Personality

Gnomes are an intelligent and restless people. They might lack the drive and ambition of other peoples, but their creativity gives them a strong ability of ingenuity. Most gnomes are content with modest lives and gaining new knowledge is seen as a hobby, but there are those who dedicate themselves fully to acquiring it.

Gnomes are naturally witty and jovial, and prefer to overcome obstacles through cunning and innovation. Ever curious, gnomes are more often drawn to adventure by a desire to see the world than by greed or hope for fame.

## Culture

After gnomes lost most love for their work under the Dominion of Solvaris, they collectively decided that, upon taking the name of [[Freeborn]] people, they would never work in another's interest only. Gnomes may take work of every kind but favor work in which they have creative or artistic control. A gnome expects some latitude of personal say in their work, and treats a task with no room for any of that as an insult. They question plans, debate motives, and challenge the end result. They do not expect their employer to bend to all their input, but they do expect to have a voice that is heard.

Gnome society heavily features art; almost all gnomes took up some form of art, whether music, painting, cooking, building, or any other form that was considered creative by the time they came of age, or else they sponsor others. Sharing artistic creations is a large part of how gnomes connect with each other and with friends.

Gnomes place little weight on living in one place together. A gnome separated from friends and family by half the continent still counts them close, so long as word can travel freely between them. This has led to the gnomes being a large part of [[The Sending Guild]]. They lean on it heavily to trade news across whatever distance separates them from scattered kin. Most gnomes count that connection as kinship enough, and see no reason to want anything more settled in its place.

## Habitat

No gnomish homeland survived the Dominion era intact, and gnomes have had no home to exclusively call their own since. Most gnomes today live concentrated in Arcanthys's larger cities.

## Naming Customs

Gnomes go by a given name and a family name, the same convention common throughout former Dominion territory. Gnomish naming carries no distinct customs, titles, or bynames beyond that.

## Relations with Other Peoples

Gnomes, in general, are a reclusive people who would rather stay out of others' affairs. Though some peoples interpret this as cowardice, it is more the case that gnomes simply have nothing at stake in the conflicts between most other peoples and, after decades of being ignored or pushed aside, are not particularly eager to fight someone else's fight.

Most people do share a specific distrust for a gnome: a gnome's close ties to [[The Sending Guild]] mean that a private conversation held in gnomish company might travel further than intended, and few other peoples ever fully forget it. Business meant to stay local is saved for a moment when no gnome is in earshot.

## History

### Age of Shaping

Legends say [[Vendrel, The Mother of Strings|Vendrel]], one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], shaped the first gnomes during the war between the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and the Fell Lords: small and quick-witted, built to work in tight, precise spaces alongside her own restless invention. As the war is said to have dragged on, Vendrel's experiments grew more dangerous, and according to legend, she began testing them directly on the gnomes she had made. The [[First Gods]] are said to have moved to stop her by force, and most gnomes were not freed of her experiments until the war's end.

### Current Age (Age of Wonders)

#### Under the Dominion

Solvarian engineers welcomed gnomish workers into their ranks early in the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion]]'s history, employing them as engineers and granting them access to [[Lumitech]], though only in a limited capacity at first. Gnomes made the most of what little access they were given, and it did not take Solvaris long to notice how far gnomish aptitude outran the narrow role it had been assigned.

Once that aptitude became clear, Solvaris moved to take full advantage of it. Gnomish workers were granted far wider access to Lumitech, but the welcome that had greeted the first engineers gave way to compulsion: gnomish communities were divided up and dispersed across Solvarian territory, assigned wherever their labor was needed most, with little regard for where a gnome or their family had lived before.

#### The Second Dawn and After

Gnomes took up the [[Freeborn]] identity alongside the rest of the Dominion's former bonded castes after [[The Second Dawn]], though no gnomish homeland remained to return to. Gnomes leaned on [[The Sending Guild]] heavily, using it to find scattered family, reestablish trade contacts, and settle wherever suited them once nothing stood in the way of the choice. Most gnomes today work exactly as they please, for whom they please, and regard that freedom as hard-won.
