A **Rootbound Elf** is a member of the branch of elven society holding that Lumitech and arcane industry are inseparable from the harm inflicted on elves under the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]]. Rather than continue to engage with the arcane infrastructure the Dominion left behind, Rootbound elves largely withdraw from it, seeking instead to slowly rebuild what connection to [[Sylvara, The Feywild|Sylvara]] can still be recovered through land, ritual, and patience.

## Origins

The Rootbound emerged after [[The Second Dawn]], when surviving elven communities took up the [[Freeborn]] identity and split over how to answer the Dominion's legacy. The Rootbound represent one half of that divide, alongside the [[Kindled Elf|Kindled]].

## Way of Life

Having returned to live in harmony with nature, Rootbound elves are commonly referred to as **Wood Elves** by other peoples.

## Relations

The Rootbound do not resent the [[Kindled Elf|Kindled]]'s choice to remain within institutions such as the [[Blessed Covenants]], treating it as a different answer to the same history rather than a betrayal of it.
