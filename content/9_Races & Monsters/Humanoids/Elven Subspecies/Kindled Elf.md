A **Kindled Elf** is a member of the branch of elven society holding that the knowledge left behind by the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]] cannot simply be abandoned, since discarding it only leaves it for someone else to misuse again. Rather than withdraw from arcane institutions, Kindled elves work deliberately within them, such as the [[Blessed Covenants]], to keep watch over that knowledge themselves and maintain what has been built from it.

## Origins

The Kindled emerged after [[The Second Dawn]], when surviving elven communities took up the [[Freeborn]] identity and split over how to answer the Dominion's legacy. The Kindled represent one half of that divide, alongside the [[Rootbound Elf|Rootbound]].
