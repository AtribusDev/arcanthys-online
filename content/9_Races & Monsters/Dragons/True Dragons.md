**True Dragons** are uncorrupted dragons, biologically the same as the day they were brought into existence. The one unique trait that all true dragons share is that they indefinitely grow stronger with age, instead of weakening like other species once they reach elderhood.


# Types of Dragons
The below is a WIP setup of how dragons map to the different planes.

Every creature's emotions feed the current of the plane it resonates with, but the effect is faint unless the creature carries unusual magical weight. Dragons are among the most magically potent beings in Arcanthys, making them the single strongest emotional influence on their resonant plane, though never its only one, since other creatures add their own faint currents besides. This influence only ever changes a plane's intensity, never its character: dragons stir existing currents, they do not remake a plane in their own image, and no plane takes on draconic traits as a result. The planes are not shaped by dragons, nor do dragons hold any control over them. Each dragon type simply gravitates toward whichever plane's temperament already most closely mirrors its own.
