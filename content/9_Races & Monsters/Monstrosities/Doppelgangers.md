**Doppelgangers** are shapeshifting monstrosities capable of assuming the exact face, voice, and mannerisms of another person. The first doppelgangers were not born but made: spawned directly by [[Ilvethra, The Unremembered|Ilvethra]] during [[The Borrowed Faces]], formed from a fraction of the Fell Lord's own power to stand in for what it erases, and given flesh to act where Ilvethra itself, sealed within [[Arcanthys]], could not.

# Physiology & Behaviour

A doppelganger holds no fixed appearance of its own. Few have ever seen one in its natural state and lived to describe it with any confidence, though the accounts that survive describe something smooth and featureless, echoing Ilvethra's own true form. Once it assumes a borrowed face, a doppelganger can hold that shape indefinitely, mimicking memory and manner well enough to deceive even close family, though never perfectly: something in the copy always sits subtly wrong, a flaw shared with Ilvethra's own gift for standing in another's place.

Doppelgangers today are not directly created or controlled by Ilvethra. Since the seals binding the Fell Lord were strengthened following [[The Borrowed Faces]], doppelgangers have propagated on their own, and most alive now are many generations removed from that original spawning. Whether Ilvethra retains any influence over its original creations, or could produce new ones again should its bindings weaken further, is not known.
