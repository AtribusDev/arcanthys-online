The **Hearth Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Rest Arcanarch]]. Its members operate inns, taverns, bathhouses, and wayhouses focused on comfort, safety, and recovery.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Hearth Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Repose]]. The brand gives covenant hosts practical command over food, drink, emotional steadiness, and restorative environments.

# History
The covenant expanded from temple-hostels into a commercial hospitality network that standardized lodging quality across most major roads and ports in [[Kaelvarra]]. Over time, covenant certification became a trusted indicator for clean kitchens, safe sleeping quarters, and neutral ground for travelers.

# Relations
The [[Hearth Covenant]] maintains formal neutrality in most conflicts and contracts with nearly every major polity. Caravans, mercenary companies, and armies routinely negotiate access to covenant kitchens and rest stations before campaigns begin.

# Business
The [[Hearth Covenant]] controls a large share of inns and taverns and sets service standards even in independent establishments. The [[Hosts Guild]] oversees lodging operations, tavern administration, and guest-care protocol, while covenant inspectors and branded hosts enforce baseline rules for sanitation, food safety, and room security.

A secondary branch led by the [[Provisioners Guild]] provides mobile provisioning for caravans, armies, and expeditionary fleets. Branded personnel can prepare and purify rations at scale, reducing dependence on long and vulnerable supply lines.

Since the spread of [[The Final Peace]], demand for long-range covenant provisioning has declined. The hospitality branch remains highly profitable, while field provisioning now serves primarily as a premium contract service for difficult theaters and rapid deployments.

## Competition
Its closest overlap is with the [[Verdant Covenant]] in food security contracts and with the [[Waybound Covenant]] on travel-route service packages.
