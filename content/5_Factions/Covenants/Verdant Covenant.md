The **Verdant Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Wilds Arcanarch]]. Its members specialize in rites tied to growth, husbandry, and natural cycles.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Verdant Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Verdure]]. This has made the [[Verdant Covenant]] a core institution in enhanced agriculture, with farmers relying on its rites to remain competitive.

# Business
The [[Verdant Covenant]] has heavy influence over agriculture. Farmers need their blessings to compete with other farmers who have them. These blessings increase yields and frequency of harvest without exhausting the soil or leaving crops overly vulnerable to seasons, pests, or drought.

The [[Verdant Covenant]] also operates a business that breeds and enhances animals. These creatures serve as pack animals that can pull more cargo over longer distances, carry riders farther with less rest, and in some cases are bred as resilient beasts of war.
