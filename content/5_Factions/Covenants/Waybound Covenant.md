The **Waybound Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Travel Arcanarch]], whose members serve in the transportation industry.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Waybound Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Wayfinding]]. This has allowed the [[Waybound Covenant]] to secure a near-monopoly on overland cargo and passenger transport.

# Business
The [[Waybound Covenant]] has a large stake in transportation and a dominant position in postal services. The Couriers Guild hires independent messengers to deliver letters and small parcels, while the Transportation Guild prepares goods for shipment and maintains trade roads.

## Competition
Its closest sustained competitor is the [[Windward Covenant]], especially on routes where sea and overland systems overlap.
