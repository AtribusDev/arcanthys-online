The **Maker Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Crafting Arcanarch]]. Its members are highly skilled artisans, tinkers, and crafters.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Maker Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Making]]. This has made the [[Maker Covenant]] central to the development and maintenance of infrastructure.

# Business
The [[Maker Covenant]] develops and maintains most infrastructure. They also produce many common goods, and their traveling tinkers mend broken equipment across [[Arcanthys]].

## Competition
Its primary commercial rivalry is with the [[Veinwright Covenant]], especially over large fabrication contracts.
