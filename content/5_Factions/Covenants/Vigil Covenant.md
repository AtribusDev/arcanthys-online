The **Vigil Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Vhal]]. Its members are highly regarded mercenaries and bodyguards known for discipline and martial skill. Taking sides undermines its commercial standing, so the [[Vigil Covenant]] prefers to serve all parties in a conflict.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Vigil Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Vigil]]. The brand reinforces both martial performance and protective rites, making the covenant a major military actor.
