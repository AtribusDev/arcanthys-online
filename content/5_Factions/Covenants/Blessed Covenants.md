**Blessed Covenants** are churches of [[The Arcanarchs]] whose members sometimes manifest a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] from their respective Arcanarch. They often hold near-monopolies over specific industries. They are located primarily on [[Kaelvarra]] and are generally treated as neutral in affairs between Kaelvarran nations.
