The **Whisper Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Whisper Arcanarch]], whose members serve in the communications industry.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Whisper Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Echoes]]. This has allowed the Whisper Covenant to become the main means of magical communication over long distances.

# Business
The Whisper Covenant offers the fastest means to get information across [[Arcanthys]]. The Chronicle Guild's whisper network spans across continents. By making use of [[Whisper Stone|Whisper Stones]], they send messages to locations regardless of any knowledge of the intended recipient, a constraint that limits most other magical means of communication. A large volume of information passes through their network as a result, which they use to fuel their newspapers, keeping them ahead of their competition.

The Linguists Guild offers translative services and aids in negotiations, allowing for complete secrecy through telepathy. This ability makes them useful for ventures outside of the established infrastructure, allowing for swift communication between individuals in the field.

## Competition
Its closest competitor is the [[Waybound Covenant]] who handle most other messages that are sent across Arcanthys.
