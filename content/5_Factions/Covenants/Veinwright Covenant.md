The **Veinwright Covenant** is the [[Blessed Covenants|Blessed Covenant]] in worship of the [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]] [[Forge Arcanarch]]. Its members are highly skilled miners, smiths, and transmusmiths.

# Blessedbrand
The [[Veinwright Covenant]] possesses the [[Brand of Veins]]. This has made the [[Veinwright Covenant]] a primary supplier of raw and processed materials, providing nations and other Blessed Covenants with essential resources.

# Business
The [[Veinwright Covenant]] provides most non-organic materials needed by other producers and holds exclusive mining rights to most major deposits in [[Kaelvarra]]. This position gives the covenant strong leverage over regional material prices.

## Competition
Its closest rival is the [[Maker Covenant]], particularly where refined materials and finished goods overlap.
