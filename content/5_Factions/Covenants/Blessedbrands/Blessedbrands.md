**Blessedbrands** are physical manifestations of an [[The Arcanarchs|Arcanarch]]. They primarily appear on members of [[Blessed Covenants]], though an Arcanarch may grant one to individuals outside a sanctum to further its agenda.

Some lineages preserve a higher chance of manifestation, but inheritance is never guaranteed. A sanctum member may serve for decades without manifesting a brand, while a recent initiate may manifest one within a single season.

# Guidebrands
[[The Guide]] can manifest a separate class of marks often called [[Guidebrands]]. Each guidebrand is unique to its bearer. Their effects resemble spellcasting, but are often less stable and harder to classify.

Blessedbrands most often appear during adolescence, though manifestation is not limited to that period. A manifested brand typically grows in size, complexity, and potency with training and focus. Some manifestations still occur without a clear cause. Sanctum members who manifest a brand usually gain access to new guild roles, contracts, and social standing within their covenant.
