The **Brand of Repose** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to rest and nurture.

# Usage
The [[Hearth Covenant]] holds control over the Brand of Repose. Its primary guild, the [[Hosts Guild]], operates across [[Arcanthys]]. The covenant does not own every inn, tavern, and kitchen, but its certification standards are widely treated as the benchmark for safe lodging and food service.

Brand-bearers are concentrated in covenant establishments, but some are deployed as contract provisioners for caravans, armies, and remote expeditions. These deployments became less common after the spread of [[The Final Peace]], which reduced reliance on large field-kitchen infrastructure.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Repose has powers that support calm, nourishment, and recovery. Common expressions include the soothing of frayed nerves, brief and restorative rest, conjured servants to ease labor, the purification of food and drink, the conjuring of food and water, and feasts that heal and fortify those who partake.
