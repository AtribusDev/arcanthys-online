The **Brand of Veins** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants magical dominion over ore veins, smelting heat, and industrial transformation.

# Usage
The [[Veinwright Covenant]] holds control over the Veins brand. Its [[Prospector's Guild]] oversees mining operations. Its [[Smelter’s Guild]] manages ore refinement and large-scale metal production. Its [[Ironwright Guild]] forges weapons, armor, and structural components used across [[Arcanthys]], supplying armies, cities, and industry alike.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Veins possesses instinctive control over metal and heat. They can heat and shape metal without tools, reinforce or weaken metallic objects through will alone, and have a limited ability to sense ore deposits.

Bearers also gain access to daily uses of spells such as _heat metal, pyrotechnics, elemental weapon_ (fire), _fabricate_, and _wall of stone_ (iron or metal variants), along with related transmutation and fire effects.
