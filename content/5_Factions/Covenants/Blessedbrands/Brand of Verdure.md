The **Brand of Verdure** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to nature.

# Usage
The [[Verdant Covenant]] holds control over the Verdure brand. Members of its [[Growth Guild]] travel through [[Arcanthys]] to help farmers improve yields. Its [[Lumi Beast Guild]] breeds and enhances working and war animals.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Verdure possesses power over the natural world. They can enhance the growth of plant life and communicate with and befriend animals.
