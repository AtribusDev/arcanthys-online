The **Brand of Vigil** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to protection of oneself and others.

# Usage
The [[Vigil Covenant]] holds control over the Vigil brand. Its guilds, the [[Wardens' Guild]] and the [[Shieldbearers' Guild]], operate across [[Arcanthys]], providing mercenaries, peacekeepers, bodyguards, and escorts.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Vigil has increased martial abilities and gains powers that protect themselves or others, including conjured armor and wards that turn aside harm, and shelters raised to hold back danger from those within.
