The **Brand of Gale** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to wind, weather, and open-water currents.

# Usage
The [[Windward Covenant]] holds control over the Gale brand. Through the [[Wind Guild]], it all but monopolizes shipping and transportation across sea and sky.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Gale possesses power over wind, weather, and sea currents. They can influence both water and wind, and the most powerful members can call brief shifts in local weather.
