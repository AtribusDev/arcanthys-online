The **Brand of Making** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to the crafting of items.

# Usage
The [[Maker Covenant]] holds control over the Making brand. Its [[Tinker's Guild]] maintains infrastructure and mends equipment. Its [[Artisan's Guild]] creates respected goods, both mundane and magical, that are known across [[Arcanthys]].

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Making possesses an instinctive command over crafting and fabrication. They can quickly identify flaws, mend damaged objects, and produce finished goods with far greater speed and consistency than any mundane artisan.
