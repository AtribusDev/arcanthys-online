The **Brand of Wayfinding** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to travel and journeying.

# Usage
The [[Waybound Covenant]] holds control over the Wayfinding brand. Through the [[Courier guild]] and the [[Transportation Guild]], it maintains a near-monopoly on overland transportation of cargo and passengers.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Wayfinding possesses powers related to movement and survival. They can magically improve travel pace with spells like Expeditious Retreat, Dimension Door, Phantom Steed, Flight, or long-distance teleportation.
