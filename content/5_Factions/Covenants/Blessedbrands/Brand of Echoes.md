The **Brand of Echoes** is a [[Blessedbrands|Blessedbrand]] that grants various magical benefits related to magical communication.

# Usage
The [[Whisper Covenant]] holds control over the Brand of Echoes. Its [[Relay Guild]] maintains an inter continental magical communication network that allow messages to be delivered near instantly. The [[Telepath Guild]] offers telepathic services to people outside of the Relay Guild.

# Powers
A bearer of the Brand of Echoes has an innate talent for persuasion and deception. They also carry the innate ability to send messages across great distances, share thoughts telepathically, converse in unfamiliar tongues, write words across the sky, and enter the dreams of others.
