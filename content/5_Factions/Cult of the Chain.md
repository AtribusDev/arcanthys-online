The **Cult of the Chain** was a previously unknown cult that broke the seal binding [[Kaldrus, The Chain Eternal|Kaldrus]], one of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]], some years after [[The Second Dawn]]. It surfaced with no prior record of its existence, unraveled quickly once Kaldrus was sealed again, and is remembered chiefly for one unexplained fact: an unusually large share of its membership was halfling.

# Relations

The cult's halfling-heavy membership remains the only confirmed connection between Kaldrus and any Freeborn people, and it unsettles most halfling communities more than it does anyone else; halflings themselves knew as little about the connection as outside observers did.

# Legacy

Since the cult's fall, an occasional halfling turns up in roles, professions, or company that sit oddly against the ordinary halfling temperament. Most who notice the pattern trace it back to the Cult of the Chain rather than to any broader change in halflings as a people.
