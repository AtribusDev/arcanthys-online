**The Blasphemies** are a set of advanced constructs, not unlike [[Warforged|warforged]]. Most of what is known about them comes from what they have voluntarily revealed to the world. They are said to be highly advanced lumitech constructs built by [[The Dominion of Solvaris]]. Their common name derives from the title they gave themselves upon first revealing themselves to the surviving world: a deliberate provocation, given that they were built by an empire that had long forbidden public worship of the gods.

# History
The first of the Blasphemies made itself known during [[The War of Two]], breaking free from beneath the earth to destroy an abomination born of the war's Mageblight flare. It ended the fight by unleashing lumitech weaponry that evaporated the abomination and everything else nearby, leaving behind the crater that scars the heart of [[Kaelvarra]] to this day. The construct went dormant at the centre of that crater, and its ruined outer plating became the foundation of the city later built upon it, [[Cinderhulk]].

More of the Blasphemies revealed themselves publicly for the first time during the negotiations that produced [[The Final Peace]], appearing in humanoid form and barring the [[Blessed Covenants]] from any part in the talks.

Since the Final Peace was declared, the Blasphemies have retreated to Cinderhulk, where they are said to occasionally grant audiences to [[The Cleansing Order]] to guide and direct them.

# Description
Aside from the giant that now serves as Cinderhulk's foundation, the Blasphemies have shown themselves to the public only once. On that occasion they appeared as slender, tall construct humanoids, resembling warforged but more alien and graceful. The giant, however, shows that some among them are covered in advanced lumitech weaponry.

How many constructs make up the Blasphemies, and how great their numbers were, remains unknown while the giant lies inert. No second intervention has ever occurred or been confirmed.
