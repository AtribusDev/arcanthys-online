**The Cleansing Order** is a militant order of paladins based in [[Cinderhulk]], with commanderies stationed across [[Kaelvarra]]. Its members hunt Mageblight and pursue those who misuse magic, and their training and vows grant them abilities that make them unusually effective against practitioners of magic. The Order traces its founding doctrine to the declaration of [[The Final Peace]] by the [[The Blasphemies|Blasphemies]].

# History

The Cleansing Order emerged from the aftermath of [[The War of Two]] and the [[The Final Peace|Final Peace]] declared by the Blasphemies in its wake. Refugees, soldiers, and witnesses of the war's Mageblight flare organized in the Blasphemies' name within a generation, forming the first commanderies of what became the Order.

# Organization

The Cleansing Order is organized as a military hierarchy. Aspirants train at a commandery until raised to Knight, the Order's working rank. Every Knight specializes in one of two disciplines: as a Blight-hunter, who locates and destroys Mageblight, or as a Warden, who investigates and apprehends those who misuse magic. A regional commandery and its Knights are commanded by a Marshal.

Marshals answer to the Unveiled, the Order's ruling council, who elect one of their own as Lord Marshal to command the Order's Marshals directly. Membership in the Unveiled carries one distinguishing mark: each has been received in audience within the giant's innermost chambers. Advancement to the Unveiled is rare, and the Blasphemies' willingness to grant a new member audience at all is treated within the Order as confirmation of that member's fitness to lead. What passes during an audience is never disclosed, even to the rest of the Order's leadership.
