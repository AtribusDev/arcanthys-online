**Lumir** is the life essence that flows through all living things. It fuels life, magic, and the world of [[Arcanthys]]. Its name is derived from its origin: the sun, [[Arcanum (Sun)]], from which it radiates and spreads outward to all [[Planes]] through the [[Material Plane]].

Lumir can be actively interacted with in various ways. [[Sorcerers]] are naturally infused with it, allowing them to channel its power instinctively to work feats of magic. [[Wizards]] study methods of manipulating localized Lumir to bend reality through structured arcane practice. [[Monks]] interact directly with the Lumir within themselves and others, often referring to it as _ki_. In some cases, Lumir may also passively infuse individuals with exceptional strength or talent.

Throughout history, many influential figures have demonstrated a strong connection to Lumir or have been significantly infused by it. However, intentionally granting or increasing a person's affinity for Lumir has proven extremely difficult. No clear pattern determines who is favored by it.
