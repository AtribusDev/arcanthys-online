**Mana** is a refined and condensed form of [[Lumir]], produced through industrial processes developed by [[Lumitech]]. Mana is Lumirs artificial compression into a stable, transportable state suitable for controlled use.

It is primarily employed as a energy medium for Lumitech, particularly in regions where ambient [[Lumir]] is scarce, unstable, or for Lumitech devices with large fuel demands.

### Nature and Properties
In its processed state, Mana appears as a dense, deep blue sparkling liquid. Unlike ambient Lumir, it does not dissipate freely, instead maintaining a high degree of energetic compression. This condition is not naturally stable, and requires continuous containment to preserve its form.

Mana must be restored to usable Lumir through devices known as Manaconverters, which reverse the compression process for integration into standard [[Lumitech]] systems.

Over time, or when left uncontained, Mana gradually degrades, shedding its condensed structure and returning to ambient Lumir.

### Containment and Instability
Mana requires specially engineered storage vessels designed to prevent evaporation, contamination, and discharge. These containers are typically layered with insulating and stabilizing materials that suppress Lumir activity inside the fluid. Damage or disturbances can destabilize the Mana, causing it to violently release its stored Lumir in a burst of electric energy that can arc through nearby metal, machines, and living beings potentially leading to [[Mageblight]].

### Historical and Industrial Context
The widespread adoption of Mana is closely associated with the expansion of [[Lumitech]] industry and [[The Dominion of Solvaris]]. Its introduction allowed for sustained operation of complex machinery in all environments.
