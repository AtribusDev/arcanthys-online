# First Draft
**Cinderhulk** is a city-state central to [[Kaelvarra]], considered the most neutral city on the continent, holding no major allegiance to any other faction. This neutrality is further reinforced by its status as home to [[The Cleansing Order]], the militant order that emerged from the [[The Blasphemies]]' declaration of [[The Final Peace]].

Cinderhulk is built on top of, against, and into the colossal [[Lumitech]] humanoid construct [[Blasphemy, Omega]]. Residents draw heat, energy, and water directly from the construct to sustain daily life. The City's Council controls Omega's planar hop function, by which the construct's arms can reach into another plane to deposit or retrieve objects. This function is used to send air vessels into another plane before they return to the material plane a set time later, greatly increasing travel speed across Kaelvarra.

## Society
The city's population descends largely from refugees displaced by the war that Omega ended. Coming under the influence of the [[The Blasphemies]]' declaration, most residents have since adopted the beliefs of the [[Blasphemy Church]], whose militant order, [[The Cleansing Order]], holds that the use of magic must be tightly restrained and Mageblight hunted wherever it appears.
