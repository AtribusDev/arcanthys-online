**Cinderhulk** is a city-state at the heart of [[Kaelvarra]], considered the most neutral city on the continent and holding no major allegiance to any other faction. This neutrality is further reinforced by its status as home to [[The Cleansing Order]], the militant order that emerged from [[The Blasphemies]]' declaration of [[The Final Peace]].

# Geography

Cinderhulk is built on top of, against, and into the colossal construct that serves as its foundation, one of [[The Blasphemies]], whose ruined outer plating forms the crater left by the final battle of [[The War of Two]]. Residents draw heat, energy, and water directly from the construct to sustain daily life.

## Society

The city's population descends largely from refugees displaced by [[The War of Two]]. Following [[The Blasphemies]]' declaration of [[The Final Peace]], most residents adopted the doctrine of [[The Cleansing Order]], which holds that the use of magic must be tightly restrained and Mageblight hunted wherever it appears. Within Cinderhulk itself, this doctrine is enforced as law: any use of magic other than that practiced by the Order is forbidden in the city.

# History

Cinderhulk rose from the crater left at the end of [[The War of Two]], where the first of [[The Blasphemies]] destroyed a Mageblight abomination born of the war's lumitech flare. The construct went dormant at the crater's center, and its ruined outer plating became the foundation of the city later built upon it.

The city's early population grew largely from refugees displaced by the war. Within a generation of [[The Final Peace]] being declared, refugees, soldiers, and witnesses of the war's Mageblight flare organized in the Blasphemies' name, forming the first commanderies of what became [[The Cleansing Order]].
