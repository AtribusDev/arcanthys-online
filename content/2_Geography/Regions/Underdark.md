**The Underdark** is the network of tunnels, mined halls, and flooded caverns that runs beneath the surface of Arcanthys, reaching under most of the world's continents rather than sitting beneath any single one. It is not a unified realm with one government or one frontier; the name describes everything below the reach of the sun, from shallow workings a dwarven clan has claimed and lit to depths no living record maps.

# Geography

## Claimed Stone and Open Dark

The uppermost Underdark, closest to the surface, is the part most travelled and most fought over. This is where dwarven holds cut their chambers, where wardholds stand over their sealed charges, and where duergar mining clans work their own seams under their own law. Below that claimed stone, the tunnels grow older, colder, and far less mapped; a hold's newest gallery may open onto passages no clan has ever charted, and dwarven miners are trained from childhood to treat any unfamiliar dark as a threat until proven otherwise.

## Waterways and Buried Rivers

Dwarven holds have never sat on stone alone. Every hold needs a way to bring timber down from the surface for its furnaces and to send ore and finished goods back up, and dwarves answered this by mastering the rivers running through and beneath their own stone, cutting new channels and damming old ones to serve that purpose. Buried rivers are also a hazard in their own right, since a seam driven deep enough is as likely to open into standing water as into open air. A hold's water-engineers are called on as often to drain a flooded gallery before it can be mined as to defend one already lost to something that has taken root in the dark.

## Flooded Galleries and the Lesser Maws

The [[Age of the Deep]] left a lasting mark on the Underdark that the receding seas never fully undid. Land that lay submerged during that age, along with caverns and buried rivers connected to it, still carries [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]]'s Drowned Spawn, seed-like growths that take root and mature into Lesser Maws: smaller echoes of the Open Maw itself, marked by a funnel-shaped descent, jagged stone teeth, and a shaft of dark water leading down. The ground around a Lesser Maw grows waterlogged and faintly organic, and a mining crew that breaks into one is trained to recognize the signs before going any further.

# History

## Age of Shaping

The Underdark's oldest structures date to the [[Age of Shaping]], when [[Baugrun, The Unsound|Baugrun]] shaped the first dwarves from its own body of living stone and served, for a time, as their first elder. After Baugrun broke with the [[The First Gods|First Gods]] and was judged and sealed alongside the surviving [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lords]] at the war's end, dwarvenkind split into the many clans it remains today, each raising a [[Wardholds|wardhold]] over one sealed remnant of that war, Baugrun's own prison foremost among them. These wardholds, and the Stonewatch clans still keeping some of them, are the oldest deliberately built structures anywhere in the Underdark.

## Age of the Baneful

As the [[Kelthrae]] spread across the surface world during the [[Age of the Baneful]], they bred the first aboleths, beholders, mind flayers, and other aberrations as servants built for war. The war's most powerful Kelthrae could not be destroyed by any means their opponents possessed, and some traditions hold that these survivors were sealed in place rather than banished outright, hidden somewhere within Arcanthys by rites older than the age itself. No surviving record says where, but the deep places are among those most commonly suspected, and the aberrations descended from the Kelthrae's own creations remain the ones most often encountered in the Underdark today.

## Age of the Deep

When the seas rose during the [[Age of the Deep]], the flooding reached far below ground as well as across it. [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]]'s Drowned Spawn began surfacing in flooded caverns and buried rivers far inland, and whole wardholds fell as garrisons built to guard a sealed prison were overrun instead by a Lesser Maw taking root in their lower halls. Dwarven accounts credit their clans as central to the wider effort that shackled the Drowning Deep again at the close of the age, and it is this era that dwarven tradition points to as the moment the scattered, hold-by-hold watch of the founding clans became the coordinated [[Stonewatch]] maintained since.

## Current Age (Age of Wonders)

Most dwarves alive today have no active Stonewatch duty in their family's recent memory, but the inheritance of the Age of the Deep has not gone away. A hold breaking new ground still occasionally cuts into a flooded seam grown up around a Lesser Maw, and every dwarven miner is trained to recognize the signs. Duergar mining clans continue to work their own seams under their own law, and where Baugrun is honored at all, it is honored by them.

# Denizens

- **[[Dwarves]]**: hold most of the Underdark's claimed and lit stone, cutting their chambers and wardholds into it and living there much as any dwarven community does elsewhere.
- **[[Stonewatch]]**: the dwarf clans still garrisoning an active [[Wardholds|wardhold]], keeping their charge quietly and sharing its details only with those fully accepted into the clan.
- **[[Duergar]]**: mining clans that hold [[Baugrun, The Unsound|Baugrun]]'s particular favor, naming it before opening a new seam and setting aside the first stone or ore drawn as tribute.
- **[[Aberrations]]**: aboleths, beholders, mind flayers, and other unnatural horrors encountered throughout the Underdark more often than almost anywhere else in Arcanthys, usually alone and driven by immediate need rather than any larger design.
- **[[Kelthrae]]**: the aberrations whose war-bred creations seeded the Underdark's other aberrations; the most powerful among them are said by some to lie sealed somewhere in the deep places still.
- **[[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]]'s Drowned Spawn**: seed-like growths that take root in flooded galleries and buried rivers connected to the [[Age of the Deep]], maturing into Lesser Maws and drawing cults devoted to Uru'gol to cultivate them as sacred ground.

# Hazards

- **Uru'gol's Drowned Spawn and Lesser Maws**: flooded galleries and buried rivers connected to the Age of the Deep remain capable of producing new Lesser Maws, drowned sites that draw further corruption to themselves.
- **The Unsound Silence**: a stretch of stone that returns no vibration or settling sound, the telltale sign of Baugrun's prison or its influence, treated by dwarves as a warning to be heeded rather than a curiosity to investigate.
- **Kelthrae-born aberrations**: aboleths, beholders, mind flayers, and other creatures descended from the Kelthrae's own creations, encountered more frequently here than almost anywhere else in Arcanthys.
