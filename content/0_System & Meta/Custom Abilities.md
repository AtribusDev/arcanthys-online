Most powerful characters, player characters and powerfull monsters, in Arcanthys have a strong source of Lumir within them. These individuals can tap into this lumir or their connection with the lumir arround them to draw more power than they'd ussually be able to, this can have major consequence just as Lumitech might have when it is over or missused.

A character can make use of one such ability once per long rest at high risk, determined by a table. If a character attempts a second use they are almost certain to end their story then and there.

When a character uses an ability they roll on the risk table listed in the ability, or the DM can determine to outcome without a roll. Often abilities specify a suggested outcome that fits as a consequence to the ability.

# Abilities

## General Abilities
General abilities have no extra requirements.

### Lumir Overdrive
**Description:**
- You force your body to burn its own Lumir, gaining impossible speed.
**Effect:**
- You regain your action and movement if spent.
**Cost:**
- 4 level of Exhaustion, that cannot be cured by magical means except for a wish spell.

## Martial Abilities

### Devastating Strike
**Description:**
- You put your all into your next melee attack
**Effect:**
- For your next melee attack you forgo the roll and treat the result of the roll as a 20.
**Cost:**
- You suffer the same damage
- Roll table

### Final Stand
**Condition:**
- You cannot be Death Scarred.
**Description:**
- -
**Trigger: **
- You can activate this effect when a attack would bring you to 0 hitpoints.
**Effect:**
- Instead of going below 0 hitpoints you gain temporary hitpoints by rolling any remaining hit dice you have, damage that would have brought you below 1 carries over to these new hitpoints. They last for one minute, cannot be removed by magical means and once the dissapear you go to 0 hitpoints. Whilst you have these temporary hitpoints you:
	- Cannot be healed by any source.
	- Can only make an action or bonus action, not both.
	- Your speed is doubled.
	- Any strength or dexterity roll or saving throws are made at advantage.
	- Are immune to the following conditions:
		- Charm
		- Frightened
		- Incapacitated
		- Paralyzed
		- Stunned
		- Unconscious
**Cost:**
- The cost of this ability is not immediate, instead it triggers once you reach 0 hitpoints.
- You gain the following effect, which can only be cured by wish:
	- **Death Scarred**:
		- You have disadvantage on death saving throw.
		- If you reach 0 hitpoints you start with a failed death saving throw
