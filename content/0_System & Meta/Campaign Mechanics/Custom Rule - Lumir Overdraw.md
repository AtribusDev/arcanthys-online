Adventurers are exceptional individuals, and in Arcanthys this is reflected in the unusually large reserves of [[Lumir]] they carry. This surplus lets them push beyond their normal limits in moments of need, calling on power they have not fully learned to control. Drawing on it too heavily carries a cost: overtaxing the body's Lumir reserve risks triggering [[Mageblight]].

# Mechanics
When a player uses a lumir overdraw option a backlash triggers at the end of their turn.

## Backlash options
Roll 2d6 when Mageblight is triggered. If Mageblight is already present in the area, the DM may increase the risk, or override the result below entirely.

| Roll  | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2     | **Aberration Spawn**: Mageblight tears open the nearby environment, spawning a deadly encounter with aberrations. They are hostile to anything but other aberrations, but their immediate focus is you.                                                                       |
| 3-4   | **Blighted Growth**: A mass of Mageblight erupts from your body. You cannot be healed or cleanse yourself, and you are restrained until an ally spends an action tearing it free.                                                                                             |
| 5-6   | **Creeping Bloom**: A lattice of Mageblight spreads beneath your skin and burrows in. You are poisoned, and your hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to your character level. Both last until you finish two long rests or are cleansed by a greater restoration. |
| 7     | **Exhausted Reserves**: As soon as the scene ends (for example, as soon as initiative ends), you gain 1 level of exhaustion.                                                                                                                                                  |
| 8-9   | **Overdrawn**: You take force damage equal to your character level in hit dice (e.g. a level 5 fighter deals 5d10). This damage cannot be reduced in any way.                                                                                                                 |
| 10-11 | **Vulnerable**: You are left exposed. You are vulnerable to all damage until the start of your next turn.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 12    | **Extinguished**: You instantly drop to 0 hit points.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## Player options
Players are heavily encouraged to come up with their own creative ways to overdraw on Lumir, below are a handful of examples.

### Casters
- **Unshackled Cast**: Cast a spell without using a spell slot, of a level you have access to.
- **Forgotten Working**: Cast a spell currently not prepared, of a level you have access to.
- **Strained Working**: Upcast a spell to one level higher than you have access to.

### Martials
- **Unspent Fury**: Use a class resource without expending it.
- **Adrenal Surge**: Gain temporary hit points equal to your level. As long as they remain, you gain the effect of freedom of movement and your speed increases by 10 feet.
- **Flawless Strike**: A single weapon attack ignores cover, concealment, and range restrictions. On a hit, the weapon's damage dice are doubled.
