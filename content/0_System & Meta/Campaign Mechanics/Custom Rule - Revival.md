**Revival** magic in Arcanthys carries real risk, both for the caster and for whoever comes back. The pull of [[The Everdeep]] is as strong as it is corrosive to the soul, and a caster who reaches into it to draw a soul back exposes themselves to that same pull. What returns is not always the whole of the person who died: the longer a soul has spent within the Everdeep, the more of itself it has already lost by the time it is recovered, in line with [[Cycle of the Soul]].

# Mechanics

## Effects on the Revived

### Option: Proficiency Erosion
Each time a character is revived, they permanently lose 1 point of proficiency bonus, stacking with every subsequent revival. This loss is permanent and cannot be reverted, and stacks below the character's normal, level-based proficiency bonus, so leveling up still raises the effective bonus, but the loss is never erased. If a character's proficiency bonus is ever reduced to 0, they cannot be revived again.

### Option: Ability Score Decay
Each time a character is revived, they permanently take a flat -2 to Constitution, plus a negative ability score increase, applied as the character chooses: either -2 to one ability score or -1 to two ability scores. This loss is permanent and cannot be reverted.

### Option: Constitution-Limited Revivals
A character can only be revived a number of times equal to their Constitution modifier. Once that number of revivals has been used, they cannot be revived again.

## Effects on the Caster

### Option: Backlash Table
When a caster performs a revival, roll 2d6 to determine what the Everdeep exacts from them in return.

| Roll (2d6) | Effect |
| ---- | ---- |
| 2 (double 1s) | The caster draws a deadly encounter with residents of [[Noctyra, The Shadowfell\|Noctyra]], hostile toward the caster and their allies. |
| 3-6 | The caster instantly suffers two levels of exhaustion. |
| 7 | The caster instantly suffers one level of exhaustion. |
| 8-11 | The caster must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 10 + half their character level, rounded down) or suffer 1d10 x their character level necrotic damage. |
| 12 | The character is revived without consequence. |
