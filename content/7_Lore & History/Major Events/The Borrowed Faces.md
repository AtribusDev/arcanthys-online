**The Borrowed Faces** is the name given to the first recorded incursion of [[Doppelgangers]] into the world: a slow, quiet infiltration of elven communities that produced the first [[Changelings]] before it was discovered and stopped.

# History

## The Substitutions

The first sign that the seals binding [[Ilvethra, The Unremembered|Ilvethra]] had weakened came not as a cataclysm but as a series of quiet substitutions. Elves across scattered communities began to notice, too late, that a parent, spouse, or friend was subtly not who they remembered, a small wrongness in manner or memory that went unquestioned until enough such wrongnesses were compared and found to share a pattern.

What had slipped through the weakened seal was not Ilvethra itself but doppelgangers, the first ever recorded, formed from a fraction of the same power Ilvethra uses to stand in for what it erases and sent to do in flesh what the Fell Lord could no longer do directly while bound. They targeted elven communities specifically, integrating themselves into elven lives by replacing loved ones, using a lesser echo of Ilvethra's own gift for standing in another's place.

## The First Changelings

From unions formed during this period between elves and doppelgangers still wearing a borrowed face, the first changelings were born.

## Discovery and Aftermath

The plot was uncovered before it could spread further, and the seals binding Ilvethra were strengthened, specifically to sever whatever hold it might otherwise have claimed over the changelings born of it.

The elves of that time could not accept what the changelings were or where they had come from, however. Rather than integrate them, elven communities shunned and exiled them, leaving changelings to build a life apart from the people whose blood they partly shared.
