**The Second Dawn**, sometimes called the _Bright Cataclysm_, _Solar Purge_, or _Day of Glass_, is the most recent world-shaping disaster in recorded history. It marks the sudden collapse of the [[The Dominion of Solvaris|Dominion of Solvaris]] and the near-extinction of humankind across much of the known world.

The event is defined by the appearance of a second sun in the sky, followed by a global surge of [[Lumir]] that resulted in widespread destruction, mass death, and lasting alterations to both the environment and the behavior of [[Lumitech|lumitech]]. The most infamous effect of this surge is known as the [[Fleshfire]]. Though widely feared and extensively studied, the precise cause of the Second Dawn remains uncertain. However, many consider the overuse of lumitech by the Dominion to be the cause.

# History
Prior to the catastrophe, the Dominion of Solvaris stood as the dominant political and arcane power of the [[Current Age]]. Its rulers oversaw vast developments in lumitech, the discipline of harnessing lumir: the radiant life energy believed to emanate from the sun.

## The Event
On [[TODO, YEARS]], witnesses across the world reported the sudden appearance of a second solar body in the sky above the [[capital of Solvaris]] at high noon. Smaller than the true sun yet vastly brighter, it emitted a harsh, colorless radiance that overwhelmed natural light. It remained in the heavens for a full day, and night did not come.

### The Radiant Surge
The second sun brought with it a wave of intense lumir that radiated across the world. Lumitech devices suffered catastrophic failures. Materials left exposed beneath its light ignited without visible flame. Those too close to active lumitech, or exposed to the second sun for too long, risked falling victim to the [[Fleshfire]].

The surge did not affect all peoples equally. Humanity suffered catastrophic losses in particular, due in large part to its widespread reliance on lumitech and concentration within the territories of the Dominion

## The Aftermath
### The Fall of the Dominion of Solvaris
Its capital was reduced to the [[Glass Wastes]], and above its ruins lingers the [[Solar Scar]], an anomaly in the sky that appears as a crack of radiant light. The Scar continues to radiate the same energy unleashed during the Radiant Surge, though at a far lesser intensity.

Other regions suffered greatly as well, transformed into unhabitable landscapes where [[Mageblight]] is rampant.

### The Worsening of Mageblight
Even after its initial devastation, the Second Dawn continues to shape the world. [[Mageblight]] is not a phenomenon unique to the Second Dawn; some hold that magic in Arcanthys has always carried this risk. Since the day the sun rose twice, however, outbreaks have grown far more frequent and severe, appearing in places once thought safe from corruption and requiring exceptional skill and care to guard against.
