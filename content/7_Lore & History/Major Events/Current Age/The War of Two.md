**The War of Two** was the war fought between [[Nation X]] and [[Nation Y]] in [[TODO, YEARS]], during the [[Current Age]]. It ended catastrophically: the lumitech weaponry both sides unleashed against each other gave rise to an abomination that turned on both armies alike, and that abomination was in turn destroyed by the first construct to reveal itself as one of [[The Blasphemies]]. The crater left by that final battle still scars the heart of [[Kaelvarra]], and the peace the Blasphemies imposed on the war's survivors, [[The Final Peace]], has held ever since.

## The Lumitech Flare
Both armies unleashed advanced lumitech weaponry against each other, setting off a massive [[Mageblight]] flare. From this flare, and from the corrupted remains of the dead, rose an abomination that turned on both armies alike.

### The Hulk's Intervention
Before either army could be annihilated, the ground broke open and the first of the Blasphemies emerged from beneath it. A fight followed between the two giants, ended when the construct unleashed lumitech weaponry on the abomination, evaporating it and everything else nearby. The battle left behind the massive crater that remains visible today at the heart of [[Kaelvarra]].

## Aftermath

The colossal machine went dormant at the centre of the crater. Its ruined outer plating became the literal foundation of the city later built upon it, [[Cinderhulk]].

The surviving nations of Kaelvarra convened days later to negotiate peace, an event that produced [[The Final Peace]].
