The **Fleshfire** is the commonly used term for the lethal phenomenon that afflicted countless creatures during the [[Second Dawn]]. It describes the rapid internal ignition of [[Lumir|lumir]], resulting in extreme pain and the complete disintegration of the body.

# Description
The Fleshfire manifests as a sudden and catastrophic saturation of lumir within a living creature. Veins and arteries blaze with radiant light beneath the skin as the victim experiences overwhelming pain. Moments later, the body burns from within without any visible flame, collapsing into a glass-like substance.

The entire process typically occurs within seconds, leaving little chance of escape or intervention.
