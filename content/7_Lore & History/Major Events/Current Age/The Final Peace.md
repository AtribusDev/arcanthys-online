**The Final Peace** is the peace declared by [[The Blasphemies]] in the negotiations that followed [[The War of Two]]. Its terms are absolute: any nation or faction that breaks it faces annihilation at the Blasphemies' hands, or abandonment to whatever Mageblight they otherwise keep in check. It has gone unbroken since it was declared.

# History

The surviving nations of Kaelvarra convened in the days after [[The War of Two]] to negotiate the peace between them. Before those terms could be settled, more constructs resembling the lumitech giant that had ended the war revealed themselves in humanoid form, under a name of their own choosing: [[The Blasphemies]]. They barred the [[Blessed Covenants]] from any part in the negotiations that produced the treaty, then declared the resulting peace final and absolute, warning that breaking it would mean annihilation at their hands, or abandonment to whatever Mageblight they otherwise kept in check.

# Enforcement

Since the Final Peace was declared, the Blasphemies have retreated to [[Cinderhulk]], where they are said to occasionally grant audiences to [[The Cleansing Order]], the militant order founded in the peace's name. The order has served as a constant reminder of the blashpemies promise.
