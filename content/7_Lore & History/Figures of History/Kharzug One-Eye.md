**Kharzug** claims to have died and returned from Gorvath's Hall, describing not the proud, communal afterlife others believe in, but a hall overcrowded with the unworthy, softened by endless feasting and hollow victories. He holds that Gorvath is displeased with this state of affairs, and preaches that only true, relentless war between orcs can purge the weak and restore honor to the afterlife itself. He is recorded as saying: "We have mistaken arrival for worth. The Hall rots with the untested."

Kharzug has since rallied followers toward a civil war he calls the Final Proving, framing it as a divine correction meant to cleanse both the living world and Gorvath's heaven.
