**The Age of Orcs** is the age where the first humanoid civilizations rose and fell, free from the influence of the demi-gods and external influences.

# History
After [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw]] was bound and the sea levels receded, vast stretches of land were laid bare. The orcs, once nomadic, took root upon these new shores, their numbers swelling as villages grew into cities. From this age of growth arose the civilization known as [[Unbroken Host|the Unbroken Host]].
