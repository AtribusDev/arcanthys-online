The **Age of the Baneful** is a span of early history remembered primarily through myth and fragmented accounts. It is generally characterized by widespread alien influence, extreme transformation of the natural world, and the absence of direct divine oversight.

# **History**

[[TODO, YEARS]]  
The Age of the Baneful began X million years ago, following the end of [[Age of Shaping|The Age of Shaping]]. By this point, the original gods had either withdrawn from the world or been imprisoned, leaving [[Arcanthys]] without clear divine guidance or protection.

During this period, unusual manifestations began appearing across [[Arcanthys]]. Their origin is disputed. Some traditions attribute them to lingering effects from the [[Age of Shaping|Age of Shaping]], while others claim they were the result of an external influence beyond the known planes. From these manifestations emerged the [[Kelthrae]], alien entities whose nature and purpose remain poorly understood. Their arrival coincides with the onset of widespread corruption and instability.

Rather than conquering territory in a conventional sense, the Kelthrae reshaped it. Landscapes were altered into unfamiliar forms, and existing life was transformed. The first [[Elementals]], [[Oozes]], [[Undead]], and [[Intelligent Plants]] originated from Kelthrae experimentation during this period.

Around X, the [[True Dragons|dragons]] entered into a pact with the spawn of the [[The Fell Lords|Fell Lord]] [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw]]. This agreement led to an alliance between dragons and [[Krakens|krakens]], despite their fundamentally different natures. Both groups appear to have viewed the Kelthrae as a threat that required a coordinated response.

The conflict that followed developed into a prolonged war. Although the Kelthrae were individually powerful, their lack of unified purpose limited their ability to respond effectively to sustained opposition.

In response, the Kelthrae created new entities adapted for conflict. Among these were the first aboleths, beholders, and mind flayers, along with other aberrations. These creations displayed increased coordination and lethality compared to earlier Kelthrae constructs.

After an extended period of warfare, the Kelthrae were defeated. The most powerful among them could not be destroyed and were instead sealed using methods believed to derive from knowledge predating the Age of the Baneful.

Following this victory, the alliance between dragons and krakens collapsed. Dragons that had consumed the essence of fallen Kelthrae began to exhibit behavioral changes, the result of lingering external influence.

Over time, these changes became more pronounced. Dragons grew increasingly possessive and developed a strong drive to accumulate and guard both material wealth and arcane knowledge. This shift marks the origin of the hoarding behavior commonly associated with dragons.

The [[Krakens]] responded to this breakdown in the alliance with open hostility. They gathered significant quantities of [[Lumir]] and used it to power a large-scale ritual intended to weaken the bindings of [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw]].

The release of this entity triggered a rapid and catastrophic rise in sea levels. Large portions of [[Arcanthys]] were submerged over a relatively short period of time, resulting in widespread destruction.

This event marks the end of the Age of the Baneful and the beginning of [[Age of the Deep|The Age of the Deep]].
