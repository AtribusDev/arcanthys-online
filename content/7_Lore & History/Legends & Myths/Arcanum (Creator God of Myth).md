**Arcanum** is the creator god of legend credited with shaping Arcanthys out of a barren void and granting it life. Nearly every surviving school of creation doctrine traces its authority back to Arcanum's act of creation during the [[Age of Shaping]], and the deity's name endures in the [[Arcanum (Sun)|sun]] that lights the world.

# The Act of Creation

According to established tradition, Arcanum traveled the cosmos and came upon the barren world of [[Arcanthys]] at the edge of an otherwise empty universe. Moved by its unrealized potential, he granted it life through an act of divine self-offering, transforming formless reality into structured existence and establishing the planes, the laws of nature, and the conditions necessary for life. The [[The First Gods|First Gods]] are held to be the first beings shaped by his hand, tasked with refining the world he had made.

A commonly recited account of the myth reads:

> Arcanum was a benevolent god who held dominion over life itself.  
> Wandering the cosmos, he discovered the barren world of Arcanthys at the edge of an otherwise empty universe.
> 
> Seeing the world's hidden potential, Arcanum chose to sacrifice himself.  
> His essence became a radiant sun, bringing warmth and life to the once-barren world.

# The Sun and the Name

The prevailing teaching holds that the sun was named in Arcanum's honor after his sacrifice, a lasting memorial to the god whose essence became its light. Through the sun, [[Lumir]], the life force of creation, continues to radiate across Arcanthys.

Not everyone accepts the order of events implied by this teaching. Some hold that the reverse is true: that the sun was known and named long before the god was given form in worship, and that Arcanum was named after it, in recognition of the life and Lumir its rays provide. Under this view, the deity did not lend the sun his name but received it, shaped by mortal belief into a god deserving of the sun's gifts. No surviving record settles the matter, and both accounts are taught side by side in different traditions.

# Worship

Arcanum's influence persists chiefly through inherited doctrine and title rather than active worship. Leaders of the [[Blessed Covenants]], for instance, carry the title Vicar Arcanum, a name that reflects the god's enduring authority even in his absence from the Material Plane.
