*The deep past of Arcanthys survives only through myth, legend, and fragments of tradition. What follows is not a verified record, but the sequence most commonly accepted: an approximation of what is believed to have happened, not a precise account of when or how.*

# The Age of Shaping
*See main article: [[Age of Shaping]]*
[[TODO, YEARS]]
- [[Arcanum (Creator God of Myth)|Arcanum]] gives his own essence to shape the young world, then charges [[The First Gods|the First Gods]] to finish it, filling it with beast, root, and season.
- Other gods arrive from beyond the First Gods' order, later remembered as [[The Fell Lords|the Fell Lords]], and shape mortals capable of thought, feeling, and choice.
- The First Gods and the Fell Lords go to war over what these new mortals are meant to be.
- Before the war's end, three Fell Lords, [[Kharox]], [[Myzra]], and [[Threxul]], are drawn to their deaths within the newly formed [[The Everdeep|Everdeep]] by the First God [[Vhalis]], a clash said to have given rise to the first [[Celestials]] and [[Fiends]]. The four dead gods are remembered together as [[The Lost Deities|the Lost Deities]].
- Mortal belief lends the First Gods a strength no god had wielded before, and the surviving Fell Lords are bound within Arcanthys.
- The First Gods withdraw from the world they saved and are not seen again, marking the end of the age.

# The Age of the Baneful
*See main article: [[Age of the Baneful]]*
[[TODO, YEARS]]
- With the First Gods gone, [[Kelthrae|the Kelthrae]] emerge and begin reshaping the land and its life, giving rise to the first [[Elementals]], [[Oozes]], [[Undead]], and [[Intelligent Plants]].
- The [[True Dragons|dragons]] pact with the Fell Lord [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]], allying with the [[Krakens|krakens]] against the Kelthrae.
- Extended war follows. The Kelthrae create the first aboleths, beholders, mind flayers, and other aberrations to fight back.
- The Kelthrae are defeated. The most powerful among them cannot be destroyed and are sealed or banished instead.
- The alliance between dragons and krakens collapses. Dragons who fed on the essence of fallen Kelthrae grow corrupted, possessive, and hoarding, the origin of dragonkind's hoarding instinct.
- In retaliation, the krakens gather [[Lumir]] and use it to break Uru'gol's bindings. Sea levels rise catastrophically across Arcanthys, ending the age.

# The Age of the Deep
*See main article: [[Age of the Deep]]*
[[TODO, YEARS]]
- Much of Arcanthys lies beneath the sea. The krakens and their Fell Lord [[Uru’gol, The Open Maw|Uru'gol]] hold dominion over the flooded world, and underwater civilizations rise.
- Uru'gol is bound again and sea levels drop, though they remain higher than before, leaving the underwater ruins found across Arcanthys today.

# The Age of Orcs
*See main article: [[Age of Orcs]]*
[[TODO, YEARS]]
- As the waters recede, the first humanoid civilizations take root on the newly bared land, chief among them the once-nomadic orcs, whose settlements grow into [[Unbroken Host|the Unbroken Host]].
- The Unbroken Host's leader, [[Kharzug One-Eye]], convinces the civilization to wage a war of ascension, so its warriors might join the war-god [[Gorvath Iron-Blood, the First Victor|Gorvath Iron-Blood]] in [[Hall of the Worthy|the Hall of the Worthy]].
- The resulting civil war crowns no victor. The warring factions grow too weak to hold their lands and are overtaken by monsters and other civilizations, ending the Unbroken Host.

# The Current Age (Age of Wonders)
*See main article: [[Current Age]]*
**2000 BA**
- Exiles driven from [[Talorin]] to [[Solkarra]] unite under [[Aethren Solivar Draal]] after defeating [[The Thirteen Sisters]]. Solvarian airships appear over Talorin soon after, marking the start of [[The Dominion of Solvaris|the Dominion of Solvaris]] as an imperial power.

**300 BA**
- The Dominion of Solvaris is near the height of its imperial power.

**0 AA**
- [[The Second Dawn]]: a second sun appears above the Solvarian capital and unleashes a global surge of [[Lumir]], bringing the [[Fleshfire]]. The capital is destroyed, becoming the [[Glass Wastes]], and the Dominion collapses. This marks the start of the Current Age.
