**Fravalis, the Pale Expanse**, is a [[Planes|plane]] of profound cold and suspended motion where change slows to a crawl and passion gutters into embers. Suffused with elemental frost and negative-adjacent stillness, Fravalis embodies cold in stasis: numbness, detachment, clarity, restraint, and the preservation of what is.

# Description
Fravalis is a realm of vast, frozen horizons and suffocating quiet. Its sky hangs in pale gradients of white, silver, and dim blue, often illuminated by distant, unmoving auroras that shimmer without warmth. No sun traverses its firmament; light lingers in the sky, cold and sourceless, granting illumination without warmth.

The landscape is dominated by endless tundras, towering glaciers, and forests of crystalline ice. Mountains rise like jagged shards of glass, their faces locked in place for millennia. Seas exist as solid expanses of frost-veined ice, beneath which dark waters barely move. Snow falls rarely, and when it does, flakes drift downward with unnatural slowness.

Sound behaves strangely in Fravalis. It carries far but feels muted, swallowed by the cold. Footsteps crunch loudly in the stillness, yet echoes fade almost immediately. Wind is uncommon; when it blows, it does so in long, low currents that carve but do not rage.

Beyond its physical nature, Fravalis is a plane of detachment, restraint, and emotional distance. Creatures that linger within it often feel their passions cool and their impulses slow. Urgency dissolves into contemplation. Rage becomes indifference; love becomes quiet appreciation; fear becomes distant awareness.

# Planar Interactions
Fravalis is sustained by restraint and the dissipation of energy, symbolizing the universal drive toward equilibrium, preservation, and emotional control.

When Fravalis accumulates an excess of energy, it becomes increasingly resonant. Time itself seems to stretch: creatures perceive the world in agonizing slow motion, every movement drawn out and deliberate. Rivers can solidify in an instant, and blizzards erupt without warning, their fury suspended and absolute. Entire regions may become frozen in a temporal arrest, landscapes held in a still, crystalline pause where change itself falters.

The plane’s influence presses outward, seeking to quiet neighbouring realities.

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Fravalis becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the Material Plane experiences deep freezes, prolonged winters, and unnatural frosts that persist even in temperate regions. Lakes solidify overnight. Crops fail not from rot, but from stillness. Cold spells are empowered.

In affected regions, emotions cool and distance grows between people. Conflicts fade, not because they are resolved, but because no one feels strongly enough to continue them. Inspiration wanes. Grief becomes numbness. Love becomes habit.

Areas of extreme cold (glaciers, mountaintops, polar expanses) may become thin points between planes, allowing passage into Fravalis.
### Quiescent
When Fravalis is quiescent, the cold doesn't bite as deep. Cold regions warm up and the water levels rise.
