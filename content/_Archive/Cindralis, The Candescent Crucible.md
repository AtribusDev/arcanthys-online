**Cindralis, the Candescent Crucible**, is a [[Planes|plane]] of ceaseless heat and kinetic energy where matter is never truly at rest. Suffused with elemental and positive-adjacent energies, Cindralis embodies heat in motion: passion, transformation, intensity, and the inevitability of change.

# Description
Cindralis is a realm filled with heat and restless energy. As many as twelve suns may hang low in its sky at any given time, bathing the plane in relentless warmth and unyielding light. The landscape is shaped by this intensity: vast basalt plains, jagged obsidian ridges, and red-stone mountains formed and reformed by constant thermal pressure. Rivers of molten stone, ores, and minerals cut through the land, endlessly reshaping it as they flow. Water rarely exists in liquid form, instead manifesting as scalding vapor or dense, suffocating mist.

Beyond its physical nature, Cindralis is a plane of passion, desire, and commitment. Creatures that linger within it often find rest difficult, if not impossible. Patience erodes, emotions intensify, and the mind is urged toward action, whether creation, transformation, or destruction. Stillness becomes uncomfortable, and hesitation feels unnatural. In Cindralis, nothing is permitted to remain unchanged for long.

# Planar Interactions
Cindralis is fueled by passion and excess energy, symbolizing the universal drive toward change, growth, and release.

When Cindralis contains an overabundance of energy, it becomes increasingly resonant. In this state, its landscapes may melt down entirely, only to be reforged into an entirely new world. Native creatures grow more volatile, and the plane’s influence presses outward against neighbouring realities.

## Crossings

## Influence on the Material Plane
### Resonant
When Cindralis becomes resonant with [[Arcanthys]], the Material Plane experiences prolonged heat waves and droughts. Fires spread unnaturally fast, volcanoes may erupt, and areas of extreme heat can become thin points between planes, allowing creatures to cross over. Fire spells become empowered.

In such regions, emotions run hotter: tempers flare, inspiration surges, and conflicts escalate with alarming speed. Even a lit forge may be enough to transfer Cindralis’s influence into the surrounding area.

### Quiescent
When Cindralis is quiescent, heat becomes less intense and more predictable. Fire spells weaken, except in the hottest climates, and emotional extremes soften, allowing rest and restraint to return.
